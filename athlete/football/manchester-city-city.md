  Philosophy: structured positional superiority — control space before controlling ball
  Pressing intensity: high — requires full squad fitness to maintain system integrity
  Tempo: controlled — City dictate pace rather than react to opponent tempo

  FALSE NINE / CENTRE FORWARD:
    City's system has functioned with and without a natural striker.
    When playing false nine: midfield overload is the tactical intent.
    When playing natural striker: direct goal threat adds a different dimension.
    Absence modifier: ×0.92 — system adapts but creative output changes

    Controls tempo, triggers press, distributes from deep.
    Absence modifier: ×0.88 — highest individual position modifier
    System coherence reduces significantly without DLP presence.

  WIDE FORWARDS (attacking width):
    City's possession system requires width to stretch defences.
    One wide forward absent: ×0.94
    Both wide forwards absent simultaneously: ×0.88 (system-level impact)

  INVERTED FULLBACKS:
    City's system uses fullbacks who invert into midfield — not traditional fullbacks.
    Absence of an inverted fullback creates structural midfield imbalance.
    Absence modifier: ×0.93 (higher than standard fullback: ×0.96)

  GOALKEEPER (sweeper role):
    City's high defensive line requires a sweeper-keeper — not a traditional GK.
    GK absence (replacing with traditional keeper): ×0.91
