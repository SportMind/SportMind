# Fan Token Revenue Intelligence

**The circular relationship between fan token success and club financial health.**
This is a genuinely novel intelligence domain — the intersection of token economics
and club commercial strategy that no other SportMind file addresses.

---

## The circular relationship

```
Fan token success
  → club revenue (partnership fees, trading share, governance revenue)
    → club investment (squad, infrastructure, commercial)
      → club performance
        → fan token demand
          → fan token success

This is not a theoretical loop — it is observable.
Clubs that generate significant fan token revenue have additional commercial income
that can be invested in squad quality, which improves performance, which improves
fan token demand, which improves token revenue.

Understanding this loop matters for long-term demand trajectory reasoning.
The loop is self-reinforcing when active — and self-weakening when broken.
```

## Revenue structure

```
THREE FAN TOKEN REVENUE STREAMS TO CLUBS:

1. INITIAL PARTNERSHIP FEE:
   One-time payment on signing the partnership agreement.
   Signal: club received a commercial injection on announcement.
   Apply: ×1.03 CDI for 6 months post-launch.
   Rationale: the fee demonstrates Chiliz's commercial valuation of the partnership,
     which is a signal of the club's perceived commercial attractiveness.

2. REVENUE SHARING ON TOKEN TRADING:
   Ongoing revenue as tokens are bought and sold on the platform.
   Actively traded tokens generate more revenue than dormant tokens.
   This creates a direct, observable link between community engagement and club income.
   Signal: clubs have a financial incentive to actively promote their tokens —
     the more their community trades, the more revenue the club receives.

3. GOVERNANCE VOTE AND POLLING REVENUE:
   Socios charges fans to participate in governance votes.
   Revenue is shared with clubs.
   Signal: active governance = active community = active revenue stream.
   Inverse signal: if a club calls many votes while under financial pressure,
     the club may be optimising token revenue over genuine community engagement.
   See: FINANCIAL CONSTRAINT + GOVERNANCE VOTE in financial-fair-play-intelligence.md
```

## Club promotion as observable proxy

```
HOW MUCH A CLUB PROMOTES ITS FAN TOKEN IS THE KEY OBSERVABLE SIGNAL
for how significant the revenue is to that club.

HEAVY CLUB PROMOTION (official app, matchday visibility, player involvement,
  first-team social media, stadium signage):
  Fan token is a priority revenue stream.
  Apply: ×1.08 demand amplifier.
  Rationale: the club is actively driving adoption — trading volume and governance
    participation will be higher, revenue will be higher, circular loop is active.

MODERATE CLUB PROMOTION (occasional mentions, some digital content):
  Fan token is a secondary product.
  Apply: ×1.00 — no additional modifier.
  Standard fan token intelligence applies without promotional amplifier.

MINIMAL CLUB PROMOTION (token exists but rarely officially acknowledged):
  Fan token is a peripheral product — revenue is not significant to the club.
  Apply: ×0.94 demand signal.
  Rationale: without club promotion, organic demand is constrained.

NO PROMOTION (token exists but club does not acknowledge it publicly):
  LEGACY_CONTRACT risk signal.
  Apply: ×0.85 demand signal.
  The partnership may be near its end — monitor for non-renewal signals.
  Reference: fan-token/official-verification-framework.md for verification process.
```

## Fan token revenue as CDI input

```
SIGNIFICANT FAN TOKEN REVENUE (material relative to overall commercial income):
  Apply: ×1.05 CDI boost.
  Rationale: the token is genuinely integrated into the club's commercial model.
    Chiliz and club both have financial incentive to maintain and grow the partnership.
    Circular loop is active — success reinforces success.

NEGLIGIBLE FAN TOKEN REVENUE (token exists but commercial contribution is minimal):
  No CDI boost — the token is not a meaningful commercial product for this club.
  The club has no strong financial incentive to drive adoption.

ESTIMATING SIGNIFICANCE WITHOUT DISCLOSED FIGURES:
  Fan token revenue is not publicly disclosed by most clubs.
  Use club promotion signal as the observable proxy:
    Heavy promotion → revenue is likely significant → apply ×1.05 CDI
    Minimal promotion → revenue is likely negligible → no CDI boost
  Do not attempt to calculate specific revenue figures.
```

## World Cup national team token revenue signal

```
ELEVATED TRADING DURING WORLD CUP:
  National team fan tokens generate significantly elevated trading volume
  during World Cup tournaments — the primary demand event for these tokens.
  Elevated trading volume directly increases federation revenue from the partnership.

FEDERATION INCENTIVE SIGNAL:
  Federations have a confirmed financial incentive to actively promote their tokens
  during World Cup — more promotion → more trading → more revenue.
  Apply: ×1.20 to governance and engagement signals during World Cup period.
  Rationale: the federation is financially motivated to keep the community active.

NON-PARTICIPATING NATION:
  If the national team token's federation does not qualify for the World Cup:
  Apply: ×0.80 demand signal for the tournament period.
  The federation loses the primary revenue amplifier event.
  Reference: athlete/national-teams/ files for qualification framework.
```

## Loop activation and disruption signals

```
CIRCULAR LOOP ACTIVE (positive):
  Heavy club promotion + rising trading volume + frequent governance votes:
  All three present: loop is self-reinforcing. Sustained ×1.08 demand amplifier.
  Monitor for loop disruption signals.

CIRCULAR LOOP DISRUPTED (negative):
  Signals that break the loop:
  · Club performance collapses (reduces fan engagement)
  · Club stops promoting token (reduces trading volume)
  · Financial constraint forces minimal commercial investment
  · Partnership non-renewal announced

  When loop disruption is confirmed:
  Apply: ×0.90 demand signal immediately.
  The self-reinforcing dynamic has become self-weakening.

LOOP REACTIVATION:
  New signing with fan token involvement, new manager with strong fan engagement,
  improved performance restoring fan confidence.
  Apply: ×1.05 recovery signal when reactivation signals are confirmed.
```

## MIND DIMENSIONS

**Intelligence:** Teaches the circular relationship between fan token success and club financial health — a novel intelligence domain not covered elsewhere in the library, with observable proxies (club promotion level) that agents can assess without disclosed financial data.

**Reasoning:** Provides the revenue structure framework, the club promotion signal as the primary reasoning input, and the loop activation/disruption framework for demand trajectory assessment.

**Context:** Applies to any long-term fan token demand trajectory analysis, any assessment of whether a club is genuinely invested in its fan token partnership, and any scenario where club financial health and fan token performance are both relevant signals simultaneously.

**Memory:** Club promotion patterns can be validated over time — clubs that heavily promote tokens should show higher engagement and demand metrics in calibration records. The relationship between promotion intensity and demand levels is a calibratable hypothesis.

**Judgment:** Do not attempt to calculate specific fan token revenue figures — these are not publicly disclosed. Use the club promotion signal as the observable proxy for revenue significance. Treat governance vote frequency during financial constraint as a potential negative signal, not always a positive one.

**Attention:** Fan token revenue intelligence is Horizon 2-3 context. It informs demand trajectory, not match-day signals. Do not apply in pre-match reasoning chains alongside adjusted score calculations.

**Execution:** Signal generation workflow, event playbooks, and structured output templates defined

**Collaboration:** Integrates with core reasoning frameworks, sport domain layer, athlete intelligence, and macro layer

**Learning:** As more data becomes available on club promotion patterns and their correlation with demand metrics, the modifier values in this framework can be refined through calibration records. The circular loop hypothesis is currently Tier 1-2 evidence (early/emerging) — not yet Tier 3 established.

**Integration:** Fan token revenue intelligence integrates simultaneously with club revenue intelligence (total commercial picture), commercial partnership intelligence (partnership health), and the fan token context bridge (connecting all sport signals to token demand). The circular loop only becomes visible when all three are held together.
