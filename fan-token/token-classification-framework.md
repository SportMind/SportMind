# Fan Token Classification Framework

**Fan tokens are digital commodities (CFTC). This file governs keeping them there.**

---

## Three-tier legal classification

```
TIER 1 - DIGITAL COMMODITY (CFTC): current fan token classification.
  Must not create equity-like claims on club revenues.

TIER 2 - PAYMENT STABLECOIN (banking): not applicable to fan tokens directly.

TIER 3 - INVESTMENT CONTRACT ASSET (SEC): fan tokens must avoid this.
  Would require registration and disclosure obligations.
```

---

## Reclassification risk signals

```
HIGH RISK - avoid:
  Revenue sharing from club profits | guaranteed financial returns |
  dividend-like distributions | voting rights on commercial decisions |
  convertibility to equity
  Agent response: RECLASSIFICATION_RISK_FLAG + x0.75 US demand suppressor

MEDIUM RISK - monitor:
  Royalty-style rewards from merchandise revenue |
  secondary market maker fees distributed to holders |
  club financial performance bonuses
  Agent response: MONITOR flag + x0.90 confidence weight

LOW RISK - generally safe:
  Governance votes on fan experience | exclusive content access |
  match attendance benefits | activity-based reward multipliers |
  duration-based loyalty bonuses (Section 404 confirmed) |
  FTP PATH_2 supply mechanics (protocol events, not yield)
  Agent response: x1.05 to x1.15 demand premium
```

---

## Agent reasoning for new feature announcements

```
STEP 1: Classify against risk register above.
STEP 2: Check for equity-like claims specifically.
  Any claim on revenues or profits -> RECLASSIFICATION_RISK_FLAG regardless of framing.
STEP 3: Cross-reference yield compliance framework.
  Section 404 concern -> apply both classification and yield compliance modifiers.
```
