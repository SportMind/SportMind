## New token launch demand profile

```
NEW TOKEN LAUNCH — FOUR PHASE DEMAND FRAMEWORK:

  Note: this file covers the initial launch period. For the full post-launch
  lifecycle (phases 1-6), see fan-token/fan-token-lifecycle/.
  The four phases below precede and feed into the lifecycle model.

PHASE 1 — PRE-LAUNCH SPECULATION (4-8 weeks before launch):
  Announcement effect — partner club or sport's fans begin speculating.
  
  Demand for EXISTING related tokens increases during this window:
    Same sport (other clubs in the same league or sport): ×1.08
    Same ecosystem (all fan tokens on Socios): ×1.03
    
  Mechanism: new launch increases ecosystem interest; existing holders benefit
  from increased platform attention; new users entering the ecosystem
  begin with existing tokens before the new token launches.
  
  Pre-launch signals to monitor:
    Official partnership announcement (primary signal)
    Club social media engagement with Socios/Chiliz content
    Socios.com teaser campaigns
    Whitelist registration open for new token

PHASE 2 — LAUNCH EVENT (days 1-7 after token goes live):
  Maximum demand concentration. The launch is the single highest demand event
  in a new token's history in absolute terms.
  
  TYPICAL LAUNCH DEMAND RANGE:
    Strong launch (Tier A club, major sport): +60-80% above pre-launch baseline
    Standard launch (mid-tier club or new sport): +40-60%
    Weak launch (niche club, low ecosystem fit): +20-35%
    
  DURATION:
    Peak demand: days 1-3
    Rapid decay begins: days 4-7
    Phase 3 begins: day 8 (settling period)
    
  WHAT DETERMINES LAUNCH STRENGTH:
    1. Club global recognition (primary factor)
    2. Sport ecosystem readiness (how engaged is the sport's fanbase with crypto)
    3. Launch timing relative to major sporting events (launching pre-tournament elevates demand)
    4. Official Chiliz/Socios marketing support for the launch

PHASE 3 — SETTLING PERIOD (weeks 2-8 after launch):
  Decay from launch peak toward stable structural baseline.
  
  NEW BASELINE FORMATION:
    Typical new baseline: 15-25% above pre-launch speculation period baseline
    (The speculation period already elevated baseline; Phase 2 peak decays
     but not fully — a structural baseline above the pre-announcement level forms)
    
  FACTORS THAT ELEVATE THE NEW BASELINE:
    First competitive event result (WIN during settling period) +5-10%
    Holders who remain are higher commitment — lower sell pressure
    
  FACTORS THAT COMPRESS THE NEW BASELINE:
    Absence of competitive events during settling period (no match signal)
    Broader crypto market in bear phase during settling period (see seasonal-intelligence.md)
    First competitive event result (LOSS) −5-10%

PHASE 4 — FIRST EVENT CATALYST:
  The first significant competitive event after launch is the most consequential
  single demand signal in a new token's history (excluding the launch itself).
  
  WHY FIRST EVENT IS UNIQUELY IMPORTANT:
    New holders have not yet experienced the token through a sporting outcome.
    The first event sets expectations and emotional anchoring for the holder base.
    Positive or negative — the first result has disproportionate impact on
    long-term holder retention and word-of-mouth demand.
    
  WIN IN FIRST MAJOR EVENT:
    Extends Phase 2-level demand for additional 2-3 weeks.
    Accelerates new baseline formation at the higher end of the 15-25% range.
    Creates immediate "this works" positive narrative for new holders.
    
  LOSS IN FIRST MAJOR EVENT:
    Accelerates decay toward the lower end of the new baseline range.
    Triggers first wave of holder disappointment — some early holders exit.
    Creates headwind for narrative-driven demand recovery.
    
  AGENT RULE:
    Always flag: what is the next competitive event for a newly launched token?
    Sequence the Phase 4 catalyst carefully in any demand signal analysis.
```

## Sport vertical readiness framework

```
HOW TO ASSESS SPORT VERTICAL READINESS FOR FAN TOKEN ADOPTION:

  STRUCTURAL READINESS SIGNALS (FIVE CRITERIA — SCORE EACH):

  1. FANBASE SIZE AND GLOBAL REACH:
     Large established global fanbase with documented digital engagement.
     Highest: 100M+ global following with active digital communities
     Moderate: 10-100M regional following
     Low: sub-10M or purely local following
     
  2. DIGITAL ENGAGEMENT HISTORY:
     Sport has existing NFT, crypto, or digital collectible activity.
     Highest: established NFT market or existing digital fan engagement
     Moderate: growing digital presence without crypto history
     Low: primarily analogue or offline fan engagement
     
  3. IDENTITY CLARITY:
     Clear club or athlete identity that holders can affiliate with.
     Highest: strong club brand with long history and distinct cultural identity
     Moderate: club exists but identity is weak or conflated with league
     Low: individual athlete identity dependent on single person's career
     
  4. COMMERCIAL PRECEDENT:
     Existing merchandise, licensing, and commercial revenue from the fan base.
     Highest: established merchandise and licensing revenue (demand proven)
     Moderate: some commercial activity but underdeveloped
     Low: little commercial fan engagement history
     
  5. REGULATORY COMPATIBILITY:
     Primary fanbase jurisdiction is fan-token-compatible.
     Highest: EU (MiCA), UAE/VARA, UK (enacted framework), Gulf (QFC, Bahrain)
     Moderate: US (CLARITY Act framework developing), APAC (varied)
     Low: jurisdictions with active crypto restrictions

  READINESS SCORE:
    5 criteria, each scored High (2) / Moderate (1) / Low (0)
    Maximum: 10 points
    
    8-10: HIGH READINESS — likely early adopter of fan tokens
    5-7:  MODERATE READINESS — will adopt when market matures
    0-4:  LOW READINESS — structural barriers to meaningful fan token adoption

HIGH READINESS SPORTS (not yet fully active — as of current library state):

  CRICKET — IPL franchise tokens:
    Readiness: HIGH (global fanbase, strong identity, commercial precedent)
    Indian jurisdiction: regulatory developing (monitor RBI guidance)
    First IPL franchise token: would be highest-demand cricket launch in history
    
  BASKETBALL — additional NBA/EuroLeague club tokens:
    Readiness: HIGH (US regulatory risk moderating with CLARITY Act)
    NBA's global reach makes any NBA team token Tier A equivalent
    
  TENNIS — individual player tokens:
    Readiness: MODERATE-HIGH (global audience, strong athlete identity)
    Note: individual athlete token framework applies (see below)
    
  AMERICAN FOOTBALL — NFL franchise tokens:
    Readiness: HIGH (massive US fanbases; CLARITY Act will unlock US market)
    First NFL team to launch: first-mover premium ×1.40

  WOMEN'S FOOTBALL — club tokens from existing men's token clubs:
    Readiness: HIGH (infrastructure exists via men's token; see womens-football.md)
    
  FORMULA 1 — driver tokens:
    Readiness: MODERATE (individual driver tokens; career-length dependency)
    Team tokens already exist ($AM, $SAUBER, $ALPINE)
```

## First mover advantage

```
FIRST MOVER FRAMEWORK:

  PRINCIPLE:
    The first fan token in a new sport vertical or geographic market captures
    a disproportionate share of demand versus subsequent entrants.
    
  FIRST MOVER CDI PREMIUM:
    First token in new sport vertical: ×1.40 CDI modifier versus later entrants
    First token in new geographic market: ×1.25 CDI modifier
    
  WHY FIRST MOVER ADVANTAGE IS DURABLE:
    1. Holder acquisition costs: first movers acquire holders when the sport's
       fanbase is new to fan tokens — later entrants compete for already-allocated
       holder attention
    2. Reference point: the first token sets expectations for the category;
       later tokens are judged against it
    3. Ecosystem familiarity: first token teaches the sport's fans how fan
       tokens work; later tokens benefit from that education but capture less
       
  IDENTIFYING FIRST MOVER OPPORTUNITIES:
    Sport has a fanbase ≥10M globally with ZERO active fan tokens on Socios.
    First club from that sport to launch = first mover premium applies.
    
  SECOND ENTRANT DYNAMICS:
    Second token in same sport vertical: ×1.15 (smaller advantage vs third+)
    Third+ entrant: ×1.00 (no first mover premium; compete on club quality)
    
  FIRST MOVER PREMIUM DURATION:
    Premium applies for: first full season of operation
    After first season: premium partially normalises as market matures
    Permanent first-mover legacy: +5-8% structural demand floor above peers
```

## Individual athlete token framework

```
INDIVIDUAL ATHLETE TOKENS — DISTINCT REASONING FRAMEWORK:

  WHY ATHLETE TOKENS DIFFER FROM CLUB TOKENS:
    Club tokens are backed by an institution with indefinite life.
    Athlete tokens are backed by an individual with a finite career.
    This creates fundamentally different demand dynamics.
    
  PRIMARY DEMAND SIGNAL — CAREER TRAJECTORY:
    For club tokens: match results drive demand week-to-week.
    For athlete tokens: career trajectory is the primary structural signal.
    
    UPWARD TRAJECTORY:
      Rising athlete: career approaching peak → demand builds
      New record, new achievement: positive demand catalyst
      National team call-up at new level: positive demand signal
      
    PEAK CAREER:
      Established at elite level → structural demand baseline (highest)
      Major title win → elevated demand spike
      
    DECLINING TRAJECTORY:
      Approaching retirement signals → demand pressure begins
      Injury history accumulation → increased career risk premium
      Form decline across multiple seasons → structural demand compression
      
  HIGHEST NEGATIVE DEMAND EVENT — RETIREMENT ANNOUNCEMENT:
    Retirement announcement is the most severe single demand event for an
    athlete token — analogous to a club being relegated.
    
    Apply: retirement_demand_cliff = −40 to −60% immediate demand drop
    Post-retirement: token demand stabilises at legacy level (10-20% of peak)
    Legacy demand driven by: historical achievement narrative, alumni events
    
  HIGHEST POSITIVE DEMAND EVENT — MAJOR PERSONAL MILESTONE:
    World record | 100th international cap | Record-breaking achievement
    Apply: milestone_demand_spike = +25-50% over 48-72h
    Note: these are transient spikes — see tier-a-clubs-framework.md milestone framework
    
  MATCH RESULT SIGNALS FOR ATHLETE TOKENS:
    Individually exceptional match performance: +5-10% 24-48h spike
    Team result: less direct than for club tokens (team result ≠ individual result)
    Injury in match: see injury-reasoning-framework.md for career impact assessment
    
  VOLATILITY NOTE:
    Athlete tokens are structurally more volatile than club tokens:
      Higher upside spikes (milestones, career peaks)
      Higher downside risk (career end, serious injury)
      No "squad depth" to absorb individual weakness
    Apply: athlete_token_volatility_premium — widen confidence intervals by ×1.25
      for all athlete token demand predictions vs equivalent club token predictions
```
