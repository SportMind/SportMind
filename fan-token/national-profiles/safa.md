## SARS PTG Burn Tax Treatment — Unresolved

```
CRITICAL UNRESOLVED ISSUE:

The South Africa SARS Draft Guide to Taxation of Crypto
Assets (July 1 2026) does NOT address the tax treatment
of PTG treasury burns.

PTG burns reduce the fan token treasury (issuer-side).
They do not directly reduce individual holder wallets.
The impact on holders is through secondary market price
effects, not a direct disposal event.

HOWEVER:
  · Whether a PTG supply event constitutes a taxable event
    for $SAFA holders is not addressed in the July 2026
    draft guide.
  · If burns are classified as creating a capital benefit
    (through supply reduction and theoretical price effect),
    SARS may determine a taxable event has occurred.
  · This classification is currently unresolved.

AGENT RULE 2 — PTG BURN TAX TREATMENT UNKNOWN:
  Do not state or assume the tax treatment of $SAFA PTG
  burns for South African holders. The SARS draft guide
  does not address this. Monitor final guide publication
  (post-August 31 2026 comment period close) for any
  clarification. Flag any SARS guidance on PTG burns as
  a HIGH PRIORITY update to this file and to
  macro/regulatory/south-africa-sars.md.
```
