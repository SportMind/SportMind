## Two-Wave FTO Structure

```
$BELG used a two-wave Fan Token Offering (FTO) structure
at launch. This is documented here because it is unique
among the national token profiles in this directory.

TWO-WAVE FTO MECHANICS:
  Wave 1: Early access / loyalty bonus eligible
    Participants in Wave 1 are eligible for the loyalty bonus
    mechanism — typically a bonus token allocation based on
    holding behaviour post-FTO.
  Wave 2: Standard public sale
    Standard FTO participation, no loyalty bonus.

UK EXCLUSION FROM LOYALTY BONUS:
  UK-based participants were EXCLUDED from the loyalty bonus
  component of the $BELG FTO.
  This is a material distinction for UK holder analysis:
  · UK holders who participated in the $BELG FTO received
    tokens at the standard price ($1.00) but without access
    to the loyalty bonus that eligible jurisdictions received.
  · This creates a different holder cost basis for UK vs
    non-UK participants from the same FTO.

AGENT RULE 1 — UK LOYALTY BONUS EXCLUSION:
  When reasoning about UK $BELG holder behaviour, apply
  the UK loyalty bonus exclusion context. UK holders
  received standard FTO tokens without loyalty bonus.
  This may affect relative holding conviction vs holders
  in eligible jurisdictions who received bonus tokens.

AGENT RULE 2 — TWO-WAVE FTO STRUCTURE:
  Two-wave FTO structures create different holder cohorts
  within the same token. Wave 1 holders (with loyalty bonus
  in eligible jurisdictions) may have different holding
  behaviour than Wave 2 holders or UK holders.
  Apply this structural context when analysing $BELG
  aggregate holder behaviour signals.
```
