## Primary Regulators

**FSC — Financial Services Commission**
Policy authority. Sets crypto regulatory framework and
licensing requirements for virtual asset service providers.
Primary source: fsc.go.kr

**FSS — Financial Supervisory Service**
Supervisory arm of FSC. Day-to-day enforcement and oversight
of VASP compliance. Works jointly with FSC on VAUPA
enforcement.
Primary source: fss.or.kr

**KoFIU — Korea Financial Intelligence Unit**
AML/CFT oversight for virtual asset service providers.
Mandatory suspicious activity reporting and transaction
monitoring are KoFIU requirements under VAUPA.
Primary source: kofiu.go.kr

**DAXA — Digital Asset Exchange Alliance**
Industry self-regulatory body. Five major exchanges:
Upbit · Bithumb · Coinone · Korbit · Gopax.
Sets industry standards and coordinates with FSC/FSS.
Not a government regulator — self-regulatory only.
Primary source: daxa.co.kr

## Legislative History

```
SOUTH KOREAN CRYPTO REGULATORY FRAMEWORK — EVOLUTION:

Pre-2020:
  No dedicated crypto legislation.
  Regulatory guidance via FSC and KoFIU circulars only.
  Crypto exchanges operating under general financial law.

2020 — Special Financial Information Act (SFIA) Amendment:
  First mandatory VASP registration framework.
  Exchanges required to register with KoFIU.
  Real-name verification accounts mandated.
  AML/CFT obligations applied to crypto exchanges.
  Unregistered VASPs subject to criminal penalties.

2021–2023:
  Major VASP registration wave. Smaller exchanges exit market
  or consolidate. DAXA formed as industry self-regulator.
  CGT legislation repeatedly delayed. Original 2023
  implementation date deferred.

2024 — VAUPA (Virtual Asset User Protection Act):
  LIVE: July 2024.
  Scope: all VASPs operating in Korea.
  Requirements: mandatory registration · segregation of
    user assets · mandatory insurance/reserves · transaction
    monitoring · suspicious activity reporting · KoFIU oversight.
  Enforcement: FSC/FSS joint oversight · criminal penalties
    for non-compliant VASPs.
  Significant step toward comprehensive investor protection.

2026 — DABA (Digital Asset Basic Act):
  Status: PENDING H2 2026 as of 2026-09-14.
  Not yet passed. Not yet in force.
  When passed: comprehensive framework covering issuance ·
    trading · custody · investor protection.
  Will supersede or supplement VAUPA.
  Fan token classification under DABA: OPEN.
  DABA passage = BINARY EVENT — escalate immediately.
```

## Current Regime — VAUPA

**Status:** LIVE — July 2024

**Scope:** All virtual asset service providers operating
in Korea, regardless of domicile.

**Core requirements:**
· Mandatory VASP registration with FSC/KoFIU
· Segregation of user assets from exchange assets
· Mandatory insurance or equivalent reserve requirements
· Continuous transaction monitoring
· Suspicious activity reporting obligations (KoFIU)
· Real-name verified bank account requirement (from SFIA 2020)

**Enforcement:**
· FSC and FSS exercise joint oversight
· Criminal penalties applicable for non-compliant VASPs
· Unregistered VASPs operating in Korea face enforcement risk

**Implications for fan token platforms:**
· Socios.com/Chiliz must have VASP registration to operate
  legally in Korea — registration status UNCONFIRMED
· Unregistered status = operational restriction or
  enforcement risk for Korean holders
· Do not assume fan token platforms have registered
  without confirmed source
