## Five-category token taxonomy

```
CATEGORY 1 — DIGITAL COMMODITIES
  Definition:  Value derived from programmatic network operation and
               supply/demand — NOT from managerial efforts of others.
  Examples:    BTC, ETH, SOL, XRP (explicitly named in release)
  Regulator:   CFTC primary jurisdiction
  Fan token:   NOT this category

CATEGORY 2 — DIGITAL COLLECTIBLES ← FAN TOKENS
  Definition:  Assets WITHOUT intrinsic economic properties, passive yield,
               or rights to future income streams.
  Explicit examples: Fan tokens, NFTs, meme coins (absent fractionalization)
  Regulator:   Neither SEC nor CFTC in primary registration capacity
  Fan token:   YES — this is the category
  Impact:      No SEC registration required for fan token issuance or
               distribution. Howey test does not apply to the token itself.

CATEGORY 3 — DIGITAL TOOLS
  Definition:  Credentials, membership tokens, utility tokens with
               functional use. No passive yield or profit rights.
  Regulator:   Neither SEC nor CFTC in primary capacity
  Fan token:   Possible overlap with utility token classification
               but Digital Collectible is the primary classification

CATEGORY 4 — STABLECOINS
  Definition:  Payment instruments redeemable 1:1 for USD. No yield.
  Regulator:   Pending GENIUS Act effectiveness
  Fan token:   NOT this category

CATEGORY 5 — DIGITAL SECURITIES
  Definition:  Tokenized versions of traditional financial instruments —
               equities, bonds, Treasury instruments.
  Regulator:   SEC full jurisdiction
  Fan token:   NOT this category (absent fractionalization)
```
