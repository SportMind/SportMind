**Royal Decree M/121 · Effective June 10 2026 · REGULATORY_CLARITY: HIGH**

---

## Framework overview

```
WHAT M/121 DOES:
  Transitions Saudi Arabia from state-controlled sports governance to a
  regulated commercial model. Prior to M/121, KSA sports clubs operated
  under Ministry of Sport direct control with no independent legal entity
  status. M/121 grants clubs formal legal entity standing, enabling:
    · Private and foreign investment in Saudi sports clubs
    · Commercial rights ownership (broadcasting, sponsorship, digital assets)
    · Licensing framework for commercial sports entities
    · Formal basis for digital asset / fan token partnerships
    · Foundation for league-level commercial structures (SPL)

WHAT M/121 DOES NOT DO:
  M/121 does not create a dedicated digital asset law.
  SAMA and CMA retain financial and securities regulation authority.
  The entity framework enables commercial partnerships —
  but each digital asset activity still requires SAMA/CMA authorisation.
```

## Centralised governance structure

```
MINISTRY OF SPORT:
  Apex regulator for all KSA sports clubs under M/121.
  Issues club licences and governs compliance.
  Sets performance standards for commercially-licensed entities.

GENERAL SPORTS AUTHORITY (GSA):
  Operational implementation of M/121.
  Issues and monitors club legal entity registrations.
  Primary interface for club commercial rights documentation.

SAUDI PRO LEAGUE (SPL):
  Commercial league vehicle. Now has enhanced mandate under M/121.
  PIF-funded clubs (Al-Nassr, Al-Hilal, Al-Ahli, Al-Ittihad) retain
  sovereign wealth fund backing alongside new commercial rights structure.
  SPL is the primary counterparty for any Chiliz/Socios partnership in KSA.

SAMA — SAUDI ARABIAN MONETARY AUTHORITY:
  Financial regulation layer. Digital assets in payment context.
  Licences payment service providers and financial intermediaries.
  Fan token proceeds and payment flows require SAMA compliance.

CMA — CAPITAL MARKET AUTHORITY:
  Securities and capital markets regulator.
  Token-as-security classification authority.
  Fan tokens: utility token treatment assumed; CMA classification required
    before any structured fan token offering to KSA investors.
```

## Private and foreign investment — what is now permitted

```
PRE-M/121 (CLOSED MODEL):
  Saudi sports clubs: government entities with no external ownership structure.
  Foreign investment: not permitted in club ownership.
  Commercial rights: held by Ministry of Sport, not clubs.
  Digital asset partnerships: no legal entity framework to support them.

POST-M/121 (OPEN COMMERCIAL MODEL):
  Private investment in club shares: ENABLED
  Foreign investment in Saudi sports clubs: ENABLED (subject to licensing)
  Commercial rights owned by clubs as legal entities: CONFIRMED
  Broadcasting rights, sponsorship, digital assets: clubs can contract directly
  Token/digital asset partnerships: legal entity structure now exists

KEY CONSTRAINT:
  Foreign investment is enabled but subject to Ministry of Sport licensing.
  Sector-specific restrictions may apply (e.g. majority ownership limits).
  PIF-affiliated clubs have a parallel sovereign wealth fund ownership layer.
  Verify current Ministry of Sport foreign ownership guidelines before
  reasoning about specific investment structures.
```

## Licensing requirements for commercial sports entities

```
CLUB LICENCE (mandatory for SPL clubs):
  Issued by: Ministry of Sport / GSA
  Requirements: financial statements, governance structure, compliance officer
  Renewal: annual
  Consequence of non-compliance: licence suspension → commercial rights suspended

COMMERCIAL ENTITY LICENCE:
  Required for: any entity operating commercial sports services in KSA
  Scope: sports agents, talent management, commercial rights agencies
  Regulator: Ministry of Commerce + Ministry of Sport (joint jurisdiction)

DIGITAL ASSET / FAN TOKEN SPECIFIC:
  M/121 provides the entity framework — not a specific digital asset licence.
  For fan token issuance: SAMA authorisation (payment layer) + CMA classification
  (securities/utility determination) both required in addition to M/121 compliance.
  Three-layer authorisation: M/121 entity + SAMA payment + CMA classification.
```

## Fan token viability — M/121 regulatory environment

```
STATUS: TRANSITIONING

WHAT M/121 ENABLES:
  Saudi sports clubs now have legal entity status to enter commercial partnerships.
  A Chiliz/Socios partnership with an SPL club now has a legal entity counterparty.
  Pre-M/121: no formal structure to hold commercial digital asset agreement.
  Post-M/121: commercial agreement can be signed, ownership rights are clear.

WHAT M/121 DOES NOT RESOLVE:
  SAMA digital asset payment regulation: still developing (no comprehensive crypto law).
  CMA token classification: utility vs security determination for fan tokens unresolved.
  The three-layer authorisation requirement creates complexity.
  Full fan token launch pathway in KSA is NOT as clear as UAE (VARA) currently.

REGULATORY COMPARISON:
  UAE (VARA):  HIGH clarity — full framework, classification confirmed, VASP licensing clear.
  KSA (M/121): MEDIUM clarity — entity framework clear, financial regulation layer developing.
  EU (MiCA):   HIGH clarity — comprehensive.
  US (CLARITY): LOW clarity — still in legislative process.

FAN TOKEN ENVIRONMENT MODIFIER:
  TRANSITIONING — not HIGH (UAE) and not LOW (US).
  Monitor for: SAMA digital asset framework publication.
  Monitor for: First SPL club Chiliz/Socios partnership announcement.
  Monitor for: CMA fan token classification guidance.
  When all three confirmed: upgrade to HIGH clarity for KSA.
```
