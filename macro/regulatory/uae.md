## Framework overview

```
WHAT THE UNIFIED FRAMEWORK DOES:
  Establishes a single, coherent virtual assets regulatory architecture
  for the UAE by aligning the Dubai VARA (emirate-level) framework with
  the Federal CMA (federal-level) framework.

  Prior to Q1 2026: VARA and the federal Securities and Commodities
  Authority (SCA) operated somewhat independently. Effective 1 January
  2026, the CMA formally succeeded the SCA under Federal Decree-Laws Nos
  32 and 33 of 2025, and coordinated requirements now apply across both
  federal and Dubai-emirate jurisdictions for token issuance. VARA
  separately clarified its own Category 1/Category 2 issuance guidance
  in April 2026.

  Key result: a token issuer can now follow a defined, predictable compliance
  pathway rather than navigating two potentially conflicting regulatory systems.

SCOPE:
  · All virtual asset issuances in UAE (federal) and Dubai (VARA)
  · Token issuers, exchanges, brokers, custodians, investment managers
  · Fan tokens explicitly covered within utility token classification —
    utility tokens sit under VARA's Category 2 issuance track (VARA
    approval required, standalone licence not necessarily required if
    issued through a licensed VARA distributor), a distinct and lighter
    pathway than Category 1 (see correction below)
  · Any Chiliz/Socios activity distributing tokens to UAE-based holders

CORRECTION — 2026-09-28 VERIFICATION PASS:
  This file previously stated that fan tokens follow the SAME three-part
  pathway (whitepaper + "ARVA opinion" + VASP licence) used for
  Category 1 assets. That is incorrect. ARVA (see below) is specifically
  the Category 1 real-world-asset-backed token classification — a fan
  token, as a utility token, sits under Category 2, a lighter and
  distinct regulatory track. See the ARVA section and Verification
  dimension below for the corrected pathway and what remains genuinely
  unconfirmed about it.
```

## Mandatory whitepaper requirement

```
REQUIREMENT: All non-exempt token issuance in UAE must be accompanied by a
compliant whitepaper, published in a single machine-readable location. VARA's
April 2026 guidance clarified that whitepapers and risk disclosures are now
treated as legally binding documents, not merely informational — misstatements
or omissions can lead to regulatory enforcement and investor claims. Issuers
may not disclaim civil liability for whitepaper content.

WHITEPAPER MUST INCLUDE:
  · Token issuer identity and legal status
  · Token purpose, utility, and classification basis
  · Rights conferred on token holders
  · Revenue model and economic structure
  · Risk factors (comprehensive disclosure)
  · Governance mechanisms
  · Smart contract details and audit status
  · Redemption and transfer mechanics (where applicable — a pure utility
    token without a redemption right would state this explicitly)

ENFORCEMENT:
  · Pre-publication review by VARA (Dubai) or CMA (federal) depending on issuer domicile
  · Material changes to token structure require whitepaper amendment and re-review.
    VARA's guidance treats a change that would move a token into a different
    category (e.g. Category 2 → Category 1) as requiring full compliance with
    the new category's requirements BEFORE the change takes effect — not
    retrofitted afterward.
  · False or misleading whitepaper: regulatory action + potential civil/criminal liability

FAN TOKEN WHITEPAPER SPECIFICS:
  For Socios/Chiliz fan tokens distributed in UAE:
    · Must document: utility nature (voting, access, rewards — not financial return)
    · Must document: supply mechanics (if FTP PATH_2 or similar burn/mint mechanism)
    · Must document: holder rights precisely
    · Must NOT imply financial return expectations (maintains utility classification)
  The FTP PATH_2 burn/mint mechanism must be accurately described in the whitepaper.
  Misrepresenting LOSS as supply-neutral (when it is a mint event) in a whitepaper
  would constitute a material disclosure error under UAE framework.
```

## ARVA — Asset-Referenced Virtual Asset (CORRECTED 2026-09-28)

```
WHAT ARVA ACTUALLY IS (per VARA's Virtual Asset Issuance Rulebook,
rulebooks.vara.ae — primary source, verified 2026-09-28):

  ARVA stands for "Asset-Referenced Virtual Asset." It is NOT a legal-
  practitioner accreditation scheme, and it is NOT a generic legal-opinion
  requirement that applies to every token type. This file previously
  defined it incorrectly throughout — that error is corrected here.

  ARVA is VARA's Category 1 token classification for virtual assets that
  reference or represent rights/claims over REAL-WORLD ASSETS — real
  estate, gold, commodities, equity, art, carbon credits, collectibles,
  or baskets of such assets (a sibling category, FRVA — Fiat-Referenced
  Virtual Asset — covers non-AED stablecoins). Category 1 issuers
  (ARVA or FRVA) require a standalone VARA Licence, must hold reserve
  assets under licensed, segregated custody, must offer redemption
  rights, and face an enhanced disclosure and audit regime (six-monthly
  independent audits of circulating tokens and reserves).

  WHERE FAN TOKENS ACTUALLY SIT:
  A fan token — a utility token providing voting/access/rewards, with no
  claim over an underlying real-world asset and no redemption-at-par
  right — does NOT fall within the ARVA (or FRVA) Category 1
  classification. It falls under VARA's Category 2 issuance track,
  which covers utility tokens generally. Category 2 issuance requires
  VARA approval prior to issuance, but — per current Tier 2 legal
  commentary — does not necessarily require the issuer itself to hold a
  standalone VASP licence if the token is issued through a VARA-licensed
  distributor; in that structure the distributor bears primary VASP
  compliance responsibility while the issuer retains whitepaper and
  disclosure obligations.

  WHAT REMAINS UNCONFIRMED (do not overstate certainty on these):
  · Whether Category 2 approval for a utility token requires a legal
    opinion analogous to the "legal opinion... confirming any explanation
    made... or information contained in a Whitepaper" that VARA's
    rulebook requires of ARVA (Category 1) issuers specifically — no
    primary-source confirmation was found either way for Category 2
    during this pass. Treat "a formal opinion may be required" as
    plausible but NOT confirmed for fan tokens specifically.
  · The precise document set VARA expects for a Category 2 approval
    submission (as distinct from the well-documented Category 1
    submission package: whitepaper, legal opinions, custody attestations,
    governance documentation).

  AGENT INSTRUCTION: Do not use the term "ARVA opinion" to describe
  anything a fan token issuer needs — that conflates the Category 1 ARVA
  classification with a generic legal-opinion step. If reasoning about a
  fan token's UAE compliance pathway, refer to "Category 2 VARA approval"
  and flag the legal-opinion question above as unconfirmed rather than
  assuming it mirrors the Category 1 requirement.

WHY THIS MATTERS FOR AGENT REASONING:
  The corrected picture is that a Chiliz/Socios fan token distributed in
  UAE likely faces a LIGHTER compliance burden than this file previously
  implied (Category 2, not Category 1/ARVA) — but also a less
  well-documented one, since public legal commentary concentrates heavily
  on the Category 1/ARVA regime (real-world-asset tokenisation is the
  UAE's current flagship use case) and says comparatively little about
  Category 2 utility-token specifics.

  When Category 2 VARA approval is confirmed for a specific token:
    Apply: UAE_CLASSIFICATION_CONFIRMED — reduce classification uncertainty modifier.
    No longer need to apply CLASSIFICATION_UNCERTAIN discount for that
    UAE-distributed token specifically.

  When VARA approval is NOT confirmed:
    Token should not be distributed in UAE under the unified framework.
    If token is distributed anyway: COMPLIANCE_RISK signal active.
```

## VARA classification framework — fan tokens

```
UTILITY TOKEN (VARA classification, Category 2):
  Fan Token treatment: classified as utility tokens ✓
  Basis: provides access to products, services, voting, rewards, without
    economic profit-sharing or security characteristics
  Licensing burden: lighter-touch than Category 1 (ARVA/FRVA) or security
    classification — VARA approval required prior to issuance; standalone
    VASP licence not necessarily required if using a licensed distributor
  Exchange listing: permitted on VARA-licensed exchanges without
    securities registration requirements
  Distribution: legal with VARA Category 2 approval + whitepaper (see
    ARVA correction above for what legal-opinion step, if any, applies)

SECURITY TOKEN (federal CMA classification):
  Fan Token treatment: NOT classified as securities under VARA ✓
  Note: federal CMA edge case guidance may differ in specific structures
  Safe harbour: utility classification maintained if token does not
    confer ownership rights, profit-sharing, or financial return expectations

PAYMENT TOKEN:
  Fan Tokens: NOT classified as payment tokens ✓
  (Fan tokens are not designed for general payment — utility classification applies)

FTP PATH_2 MECHANICS AND CLASSIFICATION:
  The burn (WIN) and mint (LOSS) mechanics of FTP PATH_2 do not convert
  a utility token into a security token, or into an asset-referenced
  (ARVA) token, under VARA classification.
  Key test: do holders receive financial returns, or a claim over a
    real-world reference asset? No to both — they receive supply dynamics.
  Supply dynamics (burn/mint) do not equal financial return, and do not
    constitute a real-world-asset reference, under UAE framework.
  Classification: UTILITY (Category 2) — confirmed under VARA framework.
```

## VASP licensing requirements

```
WHAT REQUIRES A VASP LICENCE IN UAE (DUBAI):
  · Operating a virtual asset exchange
  · Acting as a broker-dealer for virtual assets
  · Providing custody of virtual assets
  · Providing investment management for virtual assets
  · Category 1 issuance (ARVA/FRVA) — issuer itself must be VARA-licensed
  · Any distribution, exchange, or custody of virtual assets in Dubai
    generally

FAN TOKEN PLATFORM REQUIREMENTS:
  Chiliz/Socios distributing fan tokens to UAE/Dubai-based holders:
    MUST obtain VARA Category 2 approval prior to issuance (or issue
      through a VARA-licensed distributor who holds the relevant VASP
      licence — see ARVA correction above for the distributor structure).
    MUST comply with AML/CFT requirements.
    MUST apply KYC to all UAE-based holders above threshold.
    MUST follow Travel Rule for virtual asset transfers above FATF threshold.

  If Chiliz operates through a VARA-licensed partner (not direct):
    The partner VASP bears primary compliance responsibility.
    Chiliz/Socios retains whitepaper and disclosure obligations.

LICENCE CATEGORIES:
  Exchange Licence | Broker-dealer Licence | Custody Licence | Investment Licence
  Fan token platforms typically require: Exchange or Broker-dealer licence
  (if issuing directly rather than via a licensed distributor).
```
