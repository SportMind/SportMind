## Primary Regulators

**SFC — Securities and Futures Commission**
Primary regulator for virtual assets, securities tokens,
and VATP licensing. Issues and oversees all VA trading
platform licences. Maintains public register of licensed
and approved entities. Sets substance-over-form
classification guidance.
Primary source: sfc.hk

**HKMA — Hong Kong Monetary Authority**
Regulates payment tokens, stablecoins, and the banking
interface with virtual asset activity. Less directly
relevant to fan token analysis than SFC but relevant
to payment infrastructure and stablecoin-adjacent activity.
Primary source: hkma.gov.hk

**FSTB — Financial Services and the Treasury Bureau**
Policy authority and legislative framework sponsor.
Sets the direction of Hong Kong's VA regulatory strategy.
Government policy is explicitly supportive of Hong Kong
as a VA hub — FSTB is the source of that posture.
Primary source: fstb.gov.hk

**IRD — Inland Revenue Department**
Tax authority. Governs profits tax treatment of virtual
asset activity. No CGT in Hong Kong. Profits tax applies
if crypto activity deemed a trading business.
Primary source: ird.gov.hk

## Legislative History

```
HONG KONG CRYPTO REGULATORY FRAMEWORK — EVOLUTION:

Pre-2018:
  No dedicated crypto legislation.
  SFC guidance via circulars only.
  Securities tokens regulated under existing SFO where
  applicable — substance-over-form applied.

November 2018 — SFC Conceptual Framework:
  SFC issues conceptual framework for virtual asset
  portfolio managers, fund distributors and trading
  platform operators.
  Opt-in regulatory sandbox introduced.
  Early signal of substance-over-form approach.

2019–2022:
  SFC continues sandbox licensing under opt-in framework.
  Limited number of platforms licensed. Retail access
  restricted under opt-in regime — professional investors
  only on early licensed platforms.
  Government begins signalling shift toward mandatory
  licensing and retail access.

June 2023 — VATP Mandatory Licensing:
  VATP (Virtual Asset Trading Platform) regime goes live.
  Mandatory licensing for all centralised VA trading
  platforms operating in or targeting Hong Kong.
  Retail access explicitly permitted on SFC-licensed
  platforms — major liberalisation from prior regime.
  Two core licence types for VA platforms:
    Type 1 (dealing in securities) — for security tokens
    Type 7 (automated trading services) — for VA trading
    New VA-specific categories introduced under VATP
  Criminal penalties for unlicensed operation.
  SFC public register: sfc.hk — mandatory check before
  assuming any platform is licensed.

2023–2026:
  SFC actively expanding licensing perimeter.
  More entity types being brought in.
  Government VA policy remains explicitly supportive.
  Hong Kong positions as Asia's premier VA hub — distinct
  posture from Singapore (strict but permissive) and
  South Korea (high friction).
  Stablecoin regulation in development — HKMA leading.
```

## Current Regime — VATP

**Status:** LIVE — June 2023

**Scope:** All centralised virtual asset trading platforms
operating in or targeting Hong Kong.

**Core requirements:**
· Mandatory SFC VATP licence
· Retail access permitted on licensed platforms
· AML/CFT obligations under AMLO
· Investor protection requirements
· Fit and proper requirements for operators

**Retail access:**
Explicitly permitted on SFC-licensed platforms. This
distinguishes Hong Kong from earlier regimes that
restricted retail access to professional investors only.
Retail access on licensed platforms is a positive demand
signal for jurisdictions where Socios/Chiliz obtains
a licence.

**Licensing perimeter:**
SFC is actively expanding scope. More platform types and
product categories being brought in over 2024–2026.
Monitor sfc.hk for new guidance and perimeter changes.

**Enforcement:**
Criminal penalties for unlicensed operation. SFC has
powers to restrict, suspend, and revoke licences.

## CGT / Tax Framework

**Capital Gains Tax: NONE**
Hong Kong has no capital gains tax. Crypto gains from
investments held as capital assets are not taxable for
individual holders.

**Profits tax:**
Applicable if crypto activity is deemed a trading business
under IRD substance-over-form analysis. Relevant factors:
frequency of trading · holding period · nature of activity.
Applies primarily to institutional or very frequent traders —
not typical fan token retail holders.
Standard profits tax rate: 16.5% for corporations ·
15% for unincorporated businesses.
Primary source: ird.gov.hk

**GST / VAT: NONE**
Hong Kong has no GST or VAT. No transaction tax on fan
token trades.

**Agent rule:**
Apply ZERO TAX FRICTION modifier for individual Hong Kong
holders — no CGT · no GST/VAT. Verify profits tax rule
does not apply for institutional or frequent trader profiles
before applying zero friction. Primary source: ird.gov.hk.
