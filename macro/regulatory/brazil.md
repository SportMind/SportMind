## Legislative History

```
BRAZILIAN CRYPTO TAX FRAMEWORK — EVOLUTION:

IN 1.888/2019 — effective 2019:
  First crypto reporting obligation in Brazilian law.
  Monthly self-reporting for off-exchange transactions above R$30,000.
  Domestic exchanges report all transactions automatically.
  Established CPF-linked reporting framework.
  Remains foundational — referenced in all subsequent legislation.

Law 14.478/2022 — effective June 2023:
  Virtual Asset Services Providers (VASPs) framework.
  Banco Central do Brasil designated as regulator for VASPs.
  Defined "virtual assets" in Brazilian law.
  Socios.com / Chiliz required BCB VASP registration to operate
  in Brazil.

BCB Resolution 521/2025 — effective 2025:
  Monthly FX reporting obligations for VASPs.
  Required detailed reports on stablecoin purchases, sales, and
  exchanges, plus international transfers with counterparty
  identification. Capital controls and AML oversight mechanism.

PRE-JUNE 12 2026 — PREVIOUS CGT REGIME:
  Monthly threshold: R$35,000 (gains below threshold exempt)
  Progressive CGT: 15% (gains up to R$5M) · 17.5% (R$5-10M)
    · 20% (R$10-30M) · 22.5% (above R$30M)
  Cost basis method: Weighted Average (Custo Médio)
  Reporting: IN 1.888/2019 framework

Provisional Measure MP 1.303/2025 — operative June 12 2026:
  Flat 17.5% CGT on ALL crypto capital gains.
  Monthly R$35,000 exemption threshold ABOLISHED.
  Applies uniformly: domestic exchanges · foreign exchanges ·
    self-custody wallets · DeFi platforms.
  All digital asset disposals taxed regardless of size.

  LEGISLATIVE STATUS: Provisional Measure — requires congressional
    ratification within 120 days or lapses.
    October 2026 elections create ratification uncertainty.
    Government paused further crypto tax reforms until post-election
    period.
  Agent rule: Apply 17.5% as the operative rate. Verify ratification
    status before applying to live analysis. State "subject to
    ratification" in any Brazilian holder analysis.

IN RFB 2.291/2025 — effective July 1 2026:
  DeCripto reporting framework.
  Replaces IN 1.888/2019 as primary reporting mechanism.
  CARF-aligned — OECD Crypto-Asset Reporting Framework.
  Mandatory monthly reporting for all CASPs from July 2026.
  International data exchange with OECD partner countries.

DIRECTION OF TRAVEL:
  Consistent tightening since 2019. Rate trajectory: progressive
  system to flat rate. Threshold: R$35,000 abolished.
  Reporting: annual to monthly to CARF international exchange.
  Do not assume rate or threshold stability.
```

## Current Tax Framework (operative June 12 2026)

```
CGT RATE: 17.5% flat on all realised crypto capital gains
  Applied to all Brazilian tax residents regardless of transaction
  size, platform, or asset type.
  Primary fan token holder demand modifier for Brazil.

LEGISLATIVE STATUS: PROVISIONAL MEASURE — MONITOR
  MP 1.303/2025 operative but requires congressional ratification.
  If not ratified within 120 days, previous progressive regime
  (15-22.5% with R$35,000 monthly exemption) applies retroactively.
  October 2026 elections create ratification uncertainty.
  Agent rule: Apply 17.5% as current operative rate.
  State "subject to ratification" in any Brazilian holder analysis.
  Escalate to Strategy & Brainstorm if MP lapses or is amended.

THRESHOLD: NONE (under MP 1.303/2025)
  All gains taxable from the first real.
  Previous R$35,000 monthly exemption abolished June 12 2026.

COST BASIS METHOD: Weighted Average (Custo Médio)
  Mandated by Receita Federal.
  Distinct from LIFO (Italy) — do not apply Italian or UK cost
  basis methods to Brazilian holder analysis.

TAXABLE EVENTS:
  · Sale of fan token for BRL or other fiat
  · Swap of fan token for another crypto asset (permuta)
  · Use of fan token to purchase goods or services

NON-TAXABLE:
  · Purchase of fan tokens with fiat currency
  · Transfer between wallets of the same holder (no disposal)

LOSS OFFSETTING:
  Capital losses may offset capital gains in same period.
  RFB requires 5 years of supporting documentation.

REPORTING OBLIGATIONS:
  Domestic exchanges: automatic monthly reporting to RFB
  Foreign exchanges / self-custody: self-report via e-CAC portal
    by last business day of following month
  DeCripto: mandatory from July 1 2026 via e-CAC
  Annual IRPF return: declare holdings with acquisition cost R$5,000+
  Quarterly gains reporting: via GCAP software

PENALTIES:
  Under-reporting: 75% of unpaid tax
  Fraud: 150% of unpaid tax plus Selic interest until settlement

VASP REGULATION (BCB):
  Socios.com / Chiliz holds BCB VASP registration.
  Required to report all Brazilian user transactions to RFB monthly.
  Monthly FX reporting to BCB under Resolution 521/2025.

SPORTMIND MODIFIER — BRAZILIAN DOMESTIC HOLDERS:
  BRAZIL: 17.5% CGT flat (MP 1.303/2025 — subject to ratification)
  · no threshold · Custo Médio · DeCripto active
```

## PTG Burn Tax Treatment — UNRESOLVED

```
STATUS: UNRESOLVED — no Receita Federal guidance issued
as of 2026-07-31.

A PTG burn event involves a fan token holder transferring tokens
to a burn address in exchange for a platform reward (match ticket,
experience, merchandise, treasury benefit via supply reduction).

Whether this constitutes an alienação (disposal) under Brazilian
law has not been clarified by the Receita Federal.

TWO POSSIBLE INTERPRETATIONS:

1. DISPOSAL INTERPRETATION:
   Burning tokens = alienação for consideration (the reward received).
   CGT applies at 17.5% on any gain above Custo Médio cost basis.

2. NON-DISPOSAL INTERPRETATION:
   Burn for utility reward = consumption of a virtual asset, not
   a sale. No CGT event — analogous to redeeming a loyalty point.
   No capital gain crystallised.

AGENT RULE 2:
  Do not assume either interpretation applies.
  PTG burn tax treatment in Brazil is UNRESOLVED.
  When modelling Brazilian domestic holder demand for PTG
  competitions, flag this uncertainty explicitly.
  Do not apply the realisation assumption or the non-realisation
  assumption to Brazilian domestic holders.

MONITORING:
  Escalate immediately to Strategy & Brainstorm if Receita Federal
  issues guidance on virtual asset burn events or fan token PTG
  mechanics.
```

## DeCripto and Reporting Obligations

```
FRAMEWORK:    DeCripto (Declaração de Criptoativos)
LEGISLATION:  IN RFB 2.291/2025
EFFECTIVE:    July 1 2026
REPLACES:     IN 1.888/2019 (valid through June 30 2026)
ALIGNED WITH: OECD Crypto-Asset Reporting Framework (CARF)

DeCripto requires CASPs to report:
  · Crypto-to-fiat trades
  · Crypto-to-crypto swaps
  · Retail payments above threshold
  · Transfers in and out of wallets
  · Movements to unhosted/self-custody wallets

REPORTING MECHANISM:
  Domestic Brazilian exchanges (CNPJ-registered): automatic monthly
    reports to RFB via e-CAC portal
  Foreign exchanges / Chiliz Chain self-custody: Brazilian holder must
    self-report via e-CAC portal by last business day of month
  All categories now covered — opacity gap effectively closed

CARF INTERNATIONAL DATA EXCHANGE:
  Brazil is an OECD CARF participant.
  Transaction data shared internationally with CARF partner jurisdictions.
  Parallel to DAC8 in EU — different mechanism, same transparency outcome.

AGENT RULE 3 — DECRIPTO BEHAVIOURAL MODIFIER:
  Apply DeCripto as a structural compliance modifier.
  Chiliz Chain self-custody holders now face explicit self-reporting.
  Model Brazilian domestic speculative trading as more conservative
  post-July 2026 than pre-DeCripto baseline.
```
