## Fan Token Classification

```
CATEGORY:   MiCA Title II — "Other Crypto-Assets"
            (not ARTs or EMTs — lightest regulatory category)

WHAT THIS MEANS:
  · No capital requirements
  · No prudential supervision
  · No reserve requirements
  · White paper required (pre-issuance)
  · White paper must be filed with national competent authority
  · No prospectus regime applies

WHITE PAPER REQUIREMENTS:
  · Content: description of issuer, token, project, rights granted
  · Filed with: national competent authority (NCA)
  · No prior approval required — notification only
  · Liability: issuer liable for information in white paper
  · Updates required if material changes occur

EXCLUDED FROM MTICA TITLE II:
  · Utility tokens freely used for issuer's goods/services only
    (narrow exemption — does not apply to most fan tokens)
  · Tokens qualifying as financial instruments (Title I applies)
  · ARTs and EMTs (Titles III and IV apply)

Fan token agent rule: fan tokens are Title II crypto-assets.
  They are NOT financial instruments. They are NOT ARTs.
  They are NOT EMTs. Apply Title II white paper and liability rules.
```

## CASP Obligations (Title V)

```
WHAT CASP MEANS:
  Crypto-Asset Service Provider — any entity providing crypto
  services as a regular occupation or business in the EU.

WHICH SERVICES REQUIRE CASP AUTHORISATION:
  · Operating a fan token trading platform
  · Exchange of crypto-assets for fiat or other crypto
  · Custody and administration of crypto-assets on behalf of clients
  · Advice and portfolio management on crypto-assets
  · Placing of crypto-assets

AUTHORISATION PROCESS:
  · Apply to home member state NCA
  · Fit and proper test for management
  · Capital requirements apply to CASP (not to the tokens themselves)
  · Once authorised in one member state: EU-wide passport applies

PASSPORTING:
  Chiliz/Socios MFSA Malta authorisation (September 2025) grants
  the right to offer CASP services across all 27 EU member states
  without separate national authorisations.
  This is the single most significant regulatory fact for fan token
  agents reasoning about EU market access.

AGENT RULE: Any fan token trading platform offering services to EU
  retail customers must be CASP-authorised. Unauthorised operation
  in the EU is prohibited from December 2024 onwards.
```

## Market Abuse Provisions

```
STATUS: ACTIVE from December 2024

WHAT MTICA MARKET ABUSE COVERS:
  · Insider dealing in crypto-assets
  · Market manipulation of crypto-assets
  · Unlawful disclosure of inside information

APPLIES TO:
  · Crypto-assets admitted to trading on an EU trading platform
  · Persons who possess inside information about those assets

FAN TOKEN RELEVANCE:
  · Fan tokens traded on EU-regulated platforms are subject to
    MiCA market abuse rules from December 2024
  · Undisclosed match outcomes, partnership announcements, or
    supply event decisions could constitute inside information

COMPARISON — MiCA vs MARC:
  MiCA market abuse:  Active December 2024 · EU jurisdiction
  MARC (UK FCA):       Active Q4 2027 · UK jurisdiction
  These are parallel, independent frameworks. MiCA authorisation
  does not exempt from MARC and vice versa.

AGENT RULE: Any person with material non-public information
  about a fan token traded on an EU platform is subject to
  MiCA market abuse rules. This applies regardless of where
  the person is located.
```

## MiCA + EU Third-Country Ban Mechanism

```
THESE ARE SEPARATE FRAMEWORKS:
  MiCA: regulation of crypto-asset issuers and service providers
  Third-country ban (Council Regulation 2026/1844): sanctions
    instrument targeting Russia sanctions evasion

INTERACTION:
  MiCA authorisation (CASP, white paper) → does NOT affect the
  third-country ban mechanism.
  The ban targets platforms based in specific third countries —
  not MiCA-regulated EU platforms.

KEY AGENT RULE:
  Chiliz/Socios MFSA Malta authorisation insulates from the
  third-country ban mechanism because:
  1. Chiliz is based in Malta (EU member state, not third country)
  2. MFSA authorisation = operating inside EU regulatory perimeter
  3. Third-country ban targets non-EU platforms, not EU-authorised ones

  MiCA compliance is therefore a structural moat against the
  third-country ban mechanism as well as against general EU
  market access risk.
```

## 8 Agent Rules

```
RULE 1 — FAN TOKEN CATEGORY:
  Fan tokens are MiCA Title II crypto-assets. Not financial
  instruments. Not ARTs. Not EMTs. Apply Title II framework.

RULE 2 — WHITE PAPER LIABILITY:
  Issuers are liable for white paper content. Material changes
  require updates. This creates ongoing disclosure obligations
  for fan token issuers in the EU.

RULE 3 — CASP AUTHORISATION IS NON-NEGOTIABLE:
  Any entity operating a fan token trading platform for EU retail
  customers must be CASP-authorised from December 2024. No
  transitional grace period indefinitely.

RULE 4 — PASSPORTING REACH:
  One MFSA Malta authorisation = EU-wide access across 27 member
  states. Do not model EU market access as jurisdiction-by-
  jurisdiction — it is a single market.

RULE 5 — MARKET ABUSE IS ACTIVE NOW:
  MiCA market abuse provisions are not future — they have applied
  since December 2024. Do not treat EU market abuse as pending.
  Apply to any signal involving material non-public information
  about EU-traded fan tokens.

RULE 6 — MiCA ≠ MARC:
  MiCA and UK MARC are parallel, independent frameworks.
  MiCA compliance in the EU does not substitute for MARC
  compliance in the UK and vice versa.

RULE 7 — MTICA MOAT vs THIRD-COUNTRY BAN:
  MiCA authorisation insulates from EU third-country ban mechanism.
  Model this as a structural competitive moat for MiCA-authorised
  fan token platforms vs non-EU competitors.

RULE 8 — WATCH WHITE PAPER UPDATES:
  If a fan token issuer makes material changes to their token
  (new utility, supply changes, governance restructuring), a
  white paper update is required under MiCA. This is a monitoring
  signal — material changes without white paper update =
  potential MiCA violation.

RULE 9 — CHZ WHITE PAPER ≠ FAN TOKEN WHITE PAPER:
  The CHZ MiCA white paper covers the CHZ native asset only.
  Individual fan tokens require separate white paper registrations
  via MFSA notification to ESMA. Never conflate the two — they
  are distinct compliance obligations for distinct assets.

RULE 10 — INDIVIDUAL FAN TOKEN WHITE PAPER STATUS:
  Individual fan token white paper registration is IN PROGRESS
  as of 2026. Named tokens confirmed in pipeline include
  $DOJO · $POR · $ARG · $CITY · $GAL · $TRA · $MENGO · $SPURS.
  Do not assume all fan tokens have completed white paper
  registration — verify via ESMA register for current status.
  Status: PROCESS STARTED · NOT ALL TOKENS COMPLETE.

RULE 11 — WHITE PAPER REVISION SIGNAL:
  White paper revisions are required for material changes (Rule 8).
  The CHZ white paper was revised October 10 2025 following the
  Pepper8 governance proposal (supply schedule change). Apply this
  as a monitoring pattern: any confirmed Fan Token Play or supply
  mechanic change may trigger a white paper revision obligation
  for affected tokens. Monitor for white paper update filings
  when supply mechanics change on any MiCA-registered fan token.
```
