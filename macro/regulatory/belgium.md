## Tax Treatment — Normal Management Standard (0% CGT)

```
CGT STATUS: CONFIRMED · 0% contingent on normal management
  classification · Type A · no hard holding period threshold

TAX REGIME: Type A — Zero CGT under normal management standard
  Framework: SPF Finances income classification — three tiers:

  TIER 1 — NORMAL MANAGEMENT (bon père de famille):
    Tax rate: 0% — zero CGT on disposal
    Standard: holder manages crypto portfolio prudently as a
      "good family man" — long-term holding, infrequent trading,
      no leveraged speculation
    This is the most likely classification for long-term fan
      token holders with no active trading pattern
    No hard holding period threshold — behaviour standard governs
    Long-term holding reinforces Tier 1 classification but is
      not the sole determinant

  TIER 2 — SPECULATIVE INCOME:
    Tax rate: 33% + communal surcharge (~50% effective)
    Trigger: SPF Finances classifies activity as speculative —
      high-frequency trading, short-term profit-seeking,
      leveraged positions
    Classification risk is real and unquantified — no formal
      bright-line rule published by SPF Finances at library date

  TIER 3 — PROFESSIONAL INCOME:
    Tax rate: Progressive income tax up to 50%
    Trigger: crypto activity constitutes a professional or
      commercial activity — organised, habitual, profit-driven
    Least likely classification for fan token holders

  Enacted: confirmed via SPF Finances guidance · longstanding
    Belgian tax doctrine · no enacted legislation change as of
    library date
  SPF Finances enforcement: ACTIVE · increasing scrutiny of
    crypto-asset disposals

DEMAND MODIFIER: BELGIUM BEHAVIOUR BIAS
  0% CGT for normal management holders creates a structural
  incentive toward long-term, infrequent holding.
  Unlike Germany (hard 12-month threshold), Belgium has no
  single trigger date — the incentive is continuous and
  behaviour-driven throughout the holding period.
  Effects:
    · Long-term Belgian holders face 0% CGT — positive demand
      signal for patient accumulation
    · Active traders face classification risk — 33% or 50%
      effective rate acts as a friction layer
    · No 12-month exit pressure spike (unlike Germany) —
      Belgian holders do not cluster exits at a threshold date
    · Classification uncertainty introduces a modest friction
      layer that Germany's confirmed §23 EStG does not have
    · Net effect: positive demand signal for long-term holders ·
      friction signal for active traders

STANDING RULE:
  Apply BELGIUM BEHAVIOUR BIAS modifier to ALL Belgian holder
  analysis. 0% CGT under normal management is confirmed
  doctrine — Type A. Retire or update ONLY if SPF Finances
  or Belgian legislature enacts a formal crypto CGT framework
  via Tier 1 source (finances.belgium.be or belgisch-staatsblad.be).
  Do not retire on media reports or draft legislation alone —
  enacted change required.
```
