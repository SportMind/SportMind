## CARF Implementation

```
WHAT CARF IS:
  Crypto-Asset Reporting Framework (OECD). Automatic exchange of
  information between tax authorities about crypto-asset holdings
  and transactions of foreign tax residents.

SOUTH AFRICA IMPLEMENTATION:
  South Africa is the FIRST AFRICAN NATION to implement CARF.
  Effective: 2 March 2026
  First reporting period: 2 March 2026 to 28 February 2027
  Reporting deadline: by end of March 2027

WHAT IS REPORTED:
  · Crypto-asset exchanges (trades, sales, conversions)
  · Crypto-asset transfers (in and out)
  · Fiat-to-crypto and crypto-to-fiat transactions
  · Wallet holdings (in some implementations)

WHO REPORTS:
  Crypto-Asset Service Providers (CASPs) operating in South Africa
  or with South African customers must report to SARS.

KEY IMPLICATION — OFFSHORE HOLDINGS NO LONGER SHELTERED:
  South African tax residents with crypto assets held on overseas
  platforms are now subject to automatic reporting via CARF.
  The "offshore = unreported" assumption is no longer valid.

AGENT RULE: CARF compliance is mandatory for CASPs serving South
  African residents. Non-compliant platforms face enforcement risk.
  This may affect platform access for South African fan token holders.
```
