## Tax Treatment — §23 EStG (0% CGT after 12 months)

```
CGT STATUS: CONFIRMED · 0% after 12 months · BEST IN LIBRARY

TAX REGIME: Type A — Zero CGT after minimum holding period
  Framework: §23 EStG (Einkommensteuergesetz) — private sales
    income framework for crypto-assets
  Long-term (12 months+): 0% — zero CGT on disposal
    MOST FAVOURABLE crypto tax treatment in SportMind library
  Short-term (under 12 months): taxed at personal income tax
    rate (14-45% depending on bracket) — HIGH SHORT-TERM FRICTION
  Annual allowance: €600 (Freigrenze) for private sales income
    — small threshold but long-term 0% rate is the primary signal
  Enacted: confirmed via BMF guidance · §23 EStG long-standing ·
    no legislation change as of library date
  Finanzamt enforcement: active · crypto gains reporting required
    for short-term disposals

PTG BURN TAX TREATMENT: OPEN ITEM
  PTG burns result in token destruction with value received
  (or transferred). Income classification under §23 EStG:
  MONITOR — no Finanzamt guidance confirmed at library date.
  Do not encode PTG burn receipts as tax-free for German holders
  until BMF or Finanzamt guidance confirms treatment.
  Escalate immediately on any published guidance.

DEMAND MODIFIER: STRONG LONG HOLD BIAS
  0% CGT after 12 months creates the strongest structural holding
  incentive of any jurisdiction in the SportMind library.
  German holders have maximum financial incentive to hold fan
  tokens beyond 12 months before disposal.
  Effects:
    · Reduced short-term trading pressure from German holders
    · Structural accumulation behaviour — German holders hold
    · Exit pressure suppressed until 12-month threshold crossed
    · At 12-month threshold: potential exit pressure from holders
      who accumulated specifically to qualify for 0% rate
    · Net effect: demand amplifier for all tokens with significant
      German holder populations

STANDING RULE:
  Apply LONG HOLD BIAS modifier to ALL German holder analysis.
  0% CGT after 12 months is enacted law — confirmed Type A.
  Retire or update ONLY if BMF or Bundestag confirms enacted
  §23 EStG amendment via Tier 1 source
  (bundesfinanzministerium.de or bundesgesetzblatt.de).
  Do not retire on media reports or coalition agreement language
  alone — enacted legislation required.
```
