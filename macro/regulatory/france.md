## Tax Treatment — PFU (30% Flat Rate)

```
CGT STATUS: CONFIRMED · 30% flat PFU

TAX REGIME: Type C — Prélèvement Forfaitaire Unique
  Rate: 30% flat on all crypto disposal gains
    (12.8% income tax component + 17.2% social charges)
  Holding period relief: NONE — 30% applies regardless of
    holding duration
  Annual allowance: NONE equivalent
  Enacted: 2019 Finance Law · confirmed applicable to
    crypto-assets including fan tokens
  DGFiP enforcement: active · crypto gains reporting
    infrastructure in place

DEMAND MODIFIER: MODERATE-HIGH FRICTION · NO HOLDING INCENTIVE
  Unlike Germany (Type A — 0% after 12 months), French holders
  have no temporal incentive to hold fan tokens beyond any
  specific duration. 30% applies on disposal at any point.
  This creates a more uniform trading pattern with no spike at
  any holding period threshold — no German-style 12-month
  accumulation and exit behaviour.
  30% flat rate is lower than Japan (up to 55%) but higher
  than UK lower-rate taxpayers (10%) and comparable to UK
  higher-rate (18% — but UK has annual allowance).

STANDING RULE:
  Apply 30% PFU modifier to ALL French holder tax analysis.
  Rate is enacted law — no UNKNOWN modifier in force.
  Retire or update ONLY if DGFiP confirms enacted rate change
  via Tier 1 source (impots.gouv.fr). Do not retire on media
  reports or draft legislation alone.
```
