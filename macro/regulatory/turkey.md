## Legislative History

```
TURKISH CRYPTO REGULATORY FRAMEWORK — EVOLUTION:

TCMB Payment Ban — effective April 30 2021:
  Central Bank of Turkey prohibited use of crypto assets for
  payments of goods and services.
  Trading, holding, and investment remain legal.
  Payment ban remains in force as of 2026-07-31.
  Structural context: crypto is investment-only in Turkey —
  not a payment medium. This shapes holder behaviour.

Law 7518 (Capital Markets Law amendment) — effective July 2 2024:
  Primary crypto regulatory framework.
  All Crypto Asset Service Providers (CASPs) must hold SPK
  operating licence to serve Turkish users.
  Article 35/B defines legal framework for crypto assets.
  Ties crypto regulation to existing Capital Markets Law.
  Defines: "crypto asset" · "wallet" · "platform" — same
  meanings as Capital Markets Law.

SPK Communiqué III-35/B.1 — effective March 13 2025:
  Establishment and operational requirements for CASPs.
  Capital adequacy requirements. Governance standards.
  Full licensing deadline: June 2026.

SPK Communiqué III-35/B.2 — effective March 13 2025:
  Working principles and operating standards for CASPs.
  User protection requirements. Reporting obligations.

MASAK Amendment — proposed September 2025:
  Proposed giving MASAK direct powers to freeze crypto accounts
  for AML/CFT purposes.
  Regulatory posture shift: monitoring → active enforcement.
  Status: enacted as part of broader AML framework — verify
  current MASAK account freeze authority status.

AK Party Omnibus Bill — introduced March 2 2026:
  Proposed: 10% withholding tax on gains from SPK-licensed
    platforms (quarterly) · president adjustable 0-20%
  Proposed: 0.03% transaction tax on all crypto trades and
    transfers via CASPs
  Proposed: 15-40% income tax on foreign platform users
  Planning and Budget Committee: approved 0.03% transaction
    tax portion (March 4-5 2026)
  Public backlash: mass #kriptodavergiyehayır campaign
    (March 24 2026) — national trending, 24.8M users mobilised

CRYPTO TAX PROVISIONS WITHDRAWN — March 26-27 2026:
  Articles 1, 3, 4, and 5 of Omnibus Bill (entire crypto tax
  framework) WITHDRAWN from parliamentary agenda.
  Withdrawal followed negotiations between ruling AK Party
  and opposition parties.
  10% withholding tax: WITHDRAWN
  0.03% transaction tax: WITHDRAWN
  15-40% foreign platform income tax: WITHDRAWN
  No crypto CGT legislation enacted as of 2026-07-31.

CURRENT STATUS — July 2026:
  SPK licensing framework: ACTIVE
  MASAK AML enforcement: ACTIVE
  Crypto payment ban: ACTIVE
  Crypto CGT framework: NONE ENACTED
  Tax on crypto gains: theoretically taxable under general
    income tax law (progressive 15-40%) but no specific
    crypto enforcement mechanism exists.
  Direction of travel: legislation will return. Withdrawal
    was political, not permanent. Monitor for reintroduction.
```
