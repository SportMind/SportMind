## Primary Regulators

**MAS — Monetary Authority of Singapore**
Sole primary regulator. Combines central bank and financial
regulator functions. Sets all virtual asset policy, issues
licences, and enforces the PSA and securities frameworks.
MAS is the only authority relevant to fan token regulatory
analysis in Singapore.
Primary source: mas.gov.sg

**IRAS — Inland Revenue Authority of Singapore**
Tax authority. Governs income tax and GST treatment of
virtual assets. DPTs are GST-exempt since January 2020.
Business income rules for frequent/institutional traders
are set by IRAS.
Primary source: iras.gov.sg

**ACRA — Accounting and Corporate Regulatory Authority**
Corporate registry. Relevant for entity structure of
VASPs incorporated in Singapore.
Primary source: acra.gov.sg

## Legislative History

```
SINGAPORE CRYPTO REGULATORY FRAMEWORK — EVOLUTION:

Pre-2020:
  No dedicated crypto legislation.
  MAS guidance via circulars and notices only.
  Securities tokens regulated under existing SFA where
  applicable — substance-over-form applied.

January 2020 — Payment Services Act (PSA):
  First dedicated VASP licensing framework.
  DPT (Digital Payment Token) Services licensing category
  introduced — covers dealing in and facilitating exchange
  of virtual assets.
  Two licence tiers: Major Payment Institution (MPI) ·
    Standard Payment Institution (SPI).
  DPTs exempted from GST effective January 2020.
  Transitional period: existing operators allowed to
  continue under exemption while applying for licence.

2020–2023:
  MAS licensing wave. Operators apply for MPI/SPI licences.
  Majority of applicants declined or withdrew — high
  compliance bar. Select exchanges and custodians licensed.
  MAS maintains public register: mas.gov.sg/regulation/
  digital-payment-token-services.

April 2024 — PSA Amendment (PS(A)A):
  Expanded scope — stricter AML/CFT obligations.
  Broader VASP coverage — more entity types brought in.
  Enhanced consumer protection requirements.
  Tighter conduct standards for licensed DPT service providers.

30 June 2025 — DTSP Regime (Digital Token Service Provider):
  Live from 30 June 2025.
  Covers: issuance · distribution · and certain
    advisory/management services for digital tokens.
  Separate from DPT dealing licence — targets token
    issuers and distributors, not just trading platforms.
  Relevant to fan token issuers and distributors operating
    in Singapore — verify if Chiliz/Socios falls in scope.
```

## Current Regime — PSA and DTSP

**PSA (Payment Services Act) — Original:**
Live January 2020. Introduced DPT Services licensing.

**PS(A)A (PSA Amendment):**
Live April 2024. Expanded scope and tightened AML/CFT.

**DTSP Regime (Digital Token Service Provider):**
Live 30 June 2025. Covers issuance, distribution, and
certain advisory/management services for digital tokens.
Separate licensing category from DPT Services.

**Licensing categories:**
· Major Payment Institution (MPI) — high transaction
  volumes · full regulatory requirements
· Standard Payment Institution (SPI) — lower volumes ·
  lighter requirements but still licensed
· Exempted entities — specific carve-outs, not general

**MAS public register:**
mas.gov.sg/regulation/digital-payment-token-services
Verify any platform's licence status here before assuming
access or compliance.

**Enforcement:**
Criminal penalties for unlicensed DPT service provision.
MAS has powers to direct, restrict, and revoke licences.

## CGT / Tax Framework

**Capital Gains Tax: NONE**
Singapore has no capital gains tax. Crypto gains from
investments held as capital assets are not taxable for
individual holders.

**Business income rule:**
If crypto activity is deemed a trading business under
IRAS substance-over-form analysis, gains are taxable as
business income. Relevant factors: frequency of trading ·
holding period · nature of activity. Applies primarily
to institutional or very frequent traders — not typical
fan token retail holders.

**GST:**
DPTs are exempt from Goods and Services Tax (GST)
effective January 2020. Fan token trades (if classified
as DPTs) attract no GST.

**Agent rule:**
Apply ZERO TAX FRICTION modifier for individual Singapore
holders — no CGT · no GST on DPTs. Verify business income
rule does not apply for institutional or frequent trader
profiles before applying zero friction. Primary source:
iras.gov.sg.
