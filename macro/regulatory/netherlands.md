## Tax Treatment

```
TYPE: E — WEALTH TAX / HOLDING COST SUPPRESSOR

Netherlands is architecturally distinct from every other jurisdiction
in the SportMind library. The Netherlands does NOT have a disposal-
based capital gains tax on crypto. Instead, crypto holdings are taxed
annually under Box 3 (Sparen en Beleggen — Savings & Investments).

─────────────────────────────────────────────
CURRENT SYSTEM (2026–2027): Box 3 Wealth Tax
─────────────────────────────────────────────

CRYPTO CLASSIFICATION:   "Other assets" (overige bezittingen) —
                         Box 3 of Dutch income tax
TAXABLE EVENT:           Annual snapshot — January 1 holding value.
                         NOT disposal-based. No tax trigger on sell,
                         swap, transfer, or holding period completion.
EFFECTIVE TAX RATE:      ~2.16% per annum on holding value
  Calculation:           6.00% deemed investment return
                         × 36% Box 3 tax rate
                         = 2.16% effective annual wealth charge
TAX-FREE THRESHOLD:      ~€57,000–€59,357 per individual
                         (~€114,000–€118,714 for couples jointly)
DAC8 STATUS:             ACTIVE from 2026 — Belastingdienst receives
                         automatic transaction data from EU exchanges.
                         Undeclared holdings increasingly detectable.
ACTUAL RETURN OPT-OUT:   Available under transitional overbruggings-
                         wetgeving (2022–2027). Holder can file Opgaaf
                         Werkelijk Rendement if actual return < deemed
                         return. Requires documented proof.

─────────────────────────────────────────────
PROPOSED 2028 REFORM: Wet werkelijk rendement box 3
─────────────────────────────────────────────

STATUS:                  PASSED House of Representatives 12 February
                         2026 · PENDING Senate (Eerste Kamer) ·
                         Minister of Finance has signalled amendments
                         likely needed · NOT YET LAW
TARGET EFFECTIVE DATE:   1 January 2028
KEY CHANGES (if enacted):
  · Replaces deemed return with actual return taxation
  · 36% flat rate on ACTUAL returns — including UNREALISED
    capital gains (value increase in year)
  · Crypto taxed on annual appreciation even if unsold
  · €1,800 tax-free annual return threshold per person
  · Loss carryforward permitted (indefinite, above €500)
  · Liquid assets (crypto, listed equities): annual
    mark-to-market taxation
  · Real estate and qualifying startups: realisation basis only

AGENT NOTE: If enacted, 2028 reform makes the holding cost burden
  more severe for volatile assets like fan tokens — paper
  appreciation triggers tax regardless of realisation.
  Do NOT treat as enacted until Senate approval confirmed.
  Monitor Eerste Kamer proceedings closely.
```

## Demand Modifier

```
MODIFIER:           NETHERLANDS HOLDING COST SUPPRESSOR
SIGNAL DIRECTION:   NEGATIVE (annual wealth drag)
SIGNAL TYPE:        Single-signal · structural · wealth-based
MODIFIER CLASS:     STANDING SUPPRESSOR — continuous, accumulates
                    annually regardless of trading behaviour

Netherlands is a Type E (Wealth Tax) jurisdiction. The annual Box 3
charge on holding value creates a continuous structural drag on fan
token positions regardless of trading behaviour or holding duration.

EFFECTS OF BOX 3 ON FAN TOKEN DEMAND:
  · Annual tax regardless of realisation — negative demand signal
    relative to disposal-based CGT jurisdictions
  · No holding period advantage (unlike Germany 12m rule or
    Portugal 365-day rule) — holding longer does not reduce tax
    burden. Annual drag accumulates from day one of holding.
  · Tax-free threshold (~€57k) limits impact on small retail
    holders but active accumulators face continuous drag
  · No threshold clustering effect (unlike Germany LONG HOLD BIAS
    where the 12-month date drives market concentration)
  · 2028 reform if enacted adds unrealised gains exposure —
    Senate pending, not yet encoded as active modifier
  · DAC8 enforcement means non-compliance risk is effectively
    closed for exchange-held tokens from 2026

STANDING RULE:
  Apply NETHERLANDS HOLDING COST SUPPRESSOR to ALL Dutch holder
  analysis. This is Type E — wealth tax, not CGT. Never model
  Netherlands as a disposal-trigger or holding-period jurisdiction.
  Update only on Belastingdienst published guidance, enacted
  legislation (officielebekendmakingen.nl), or Senate approval of
  Wet werkelijk rendement box 3.

TYPE E vs OTHER TYPES — CRITICAL DISTINCTIONS:
  Germany (Type A): disposal-based · 0% after 12 months · hard
    threshold → LONG HOLD BIAS · threshold clustering effect
  Belgium (Type A): disposal-based · 0% under bon père de famille
    standard · no threshold · BEHAVIOUR BIAS
  France (Type C): disposal-based · 30% flat PFU · no holding relief
  Netherlands (Type E): NOT disposal-based · annual wealth charge ·
    no holding period advantage · negative structural drag
  NEVER treat Netherlands as CGT jurisdiction.
  NEVER apply Germany LONG HOLD BIAS framework to Dutch holders.
  NEVER apply Belgium BEHAVIOUR BIAS framework to Dutch holders.
```
