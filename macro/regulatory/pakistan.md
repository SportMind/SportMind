## Current regulatory framework

### PVARA licensing requirement

```
REQUIREMENT:        All VASPs must obtain formal licence before offering
                    services in Pakistan (Virtual Assets Act 2026)
VASP DEFINITION:    Any person who, as a business, provides one or more
                    virtual asset services to third parties on a professional
                    basis, in or from Pakistan
ISSUER DEFINITION:  Any legal person that offers, originates, or distributes,
                    on its own behalf, a virtual asset in or from Pakistan
EXTRATERRITORIAL:   PVARA can exercise enforcement powers beyond Pakistan's
                    borders. Services conducted outside Pakistan but targeted
                    at Pakistani users may fall within scope. Per Tier 2
                    reporting (thepaypers.com, 2026-08-24): PVARA considers
                    a provider in scope if it markets to or solicits
                    Pakistani customers, onboards Pakistan-based users, or
                    supports PKR payment rails — mere accessibility of a
                    website/app from Pakistan is not sufficient if the firm
                    does not target Pakistani customers and takes
                    reasonable steps to prevent Pakistani onboarding.
                    (Section 4, Virtual Assets Act 2026)
LICENSING PROCESS:  Two-stage: (1) No Objection Certificate (NOC) before
                    incorporation (2) Full licence after — fixed sequence
                    confirmed by Tier 2 sources: NOC issued → Financial
                    Monitoring Unit registration → local incorporation
                    under Companies Act 2017 → full licence application
NOC PORTAL:         LIVE as of August 21-22 2026 (sources vary by one day
                    on the exact portal-open date; regulations formally
                    issued August 21, portal opened the following day per
                    thepaypers.com) — pvara.gov.pk/licensing
CONFIRMED NOC HOLDERS: Binance and HTX — both received early/preliminary
                    NOCs in December 2025, ahead of the general September
                    5 transitional deadline (multiple Tier 2 sources
                    corroborate independently: CoinGape, The Block,
                    Decrypt). PVARA's Chairman told the Senate (September
                    1 2026) both firms had until September 5 to complete
                    registration. No other firm's NOC status has been
                    publicly confirmed by name as of 2026-09-28.
PENALTIES:          Unlicensed operation: up to PKR 50M ($180K, current
                    exchange-rate-adjusted figure per 2026-09-05 Tier 2
                    reporting) + 5 years imprisonment. Unauthorised VA
                    offering/promotion: up to PKR 25M ($89K) + 3 years
                    imprisonment. Confirmed as a criminal offence under
                    Section 70 specifically for continued unlicensed
                    operation after the deadline (not merely a civil
                    penalty).
PUBLIC NOC LIST:    STILL NOT MAINTAINED as of 2026-09-28 (re-confirmed
                    this pass — see HP-13 section above). PVARA does not
                    publish a list of NOC holders beyond the two
                    voluntarily-announced early cases (Binance, HTX).
                    Verification of any other firm's status, including
                    Socios.com/Chiliz, is not possible independently.
```
