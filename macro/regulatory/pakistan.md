## Current regulatory framework

### PVARA licensing requirement

```
REQUIREMENT:        All VASPs must obtain formal licence before offering
                    services in Pakistan (Virtual Assets Act 2026)
VASP DEFINITION:    Any person who, as a business, provides one or more
                    virtual asset services to third parties on a professional
                    basis, in or from Pakistan
ISSUER DEFINITION:  Any legal person that offers, originates, or distributes,
                    on its own behalf, a virtual asset in or from Pakistan
EXTRATERRITORIAL:   PVARA can exercise enforcement powers beyond Pakistan's
                    borders. Services conducted outside Pakistan but targeted
                    at Pakistani users may fall within scope.
                    (Section 4, Virtual Assets Act 2026)
LICENSING PROCESS:  Two-stage: (1) No Objection Certificate (NOC) before
                    incorporation (2) Full licence after
NOC PORTAL:         LIVE as of August 21 2026 — pvara.gov.pk/licensing
PENALTIES:          Unlicensed operation: up to PKR 50M ($179K) + 5 years
                    imprisonment. Unauthorised VA offering/promotion:
                    up to PKR 25M ($89K) + 3 years imprisonment.
PUBLIC NOC LIST:    NOT MAINTAINED — PVARA does not publish a list of
                    NOC holders. Verification not possible independently.
```

### Substance-over-form classification

```
PRINCIPLE:          PVARA classifies assets by economic function and
                    real-world effect — NOT by label.
RISK TO FAN TOKENS: Calling a token a "utility token," "collectible," or
                    "governance token" does NOT automatically exempt it
                    from regulation.
STATUTORY BASIS:    Section 9(f), Virtual Assets Act 2026 — PVARA has
                    explicit power to assess any asset based on its
                    substantive features, irrespective of name.
FAN TOKEN STATUS:   UNCONFIRMED whether fan tokens fall within PVARA's
                    asset classification scope. No specific PVARA guidance
                    on fan tokens issued as of 2026-08-24.
AGENT RULE:         Do not assume fan tokens are exempt from PVARA scope
                    based on labelling. Monitor for classification guidance.
```
