    4. International engagement — hosting major events attracts global attention

    Once a government commits to a sports strategy: the signal is sustained indefinitely
    or until confirmed policy reversal.

## Government sanctions and geopolitical signals

```
SPORTS SANCTIONS — COMPETITION BANS:

  EUROPEAN COMPETITION BAN FOR A CLUB:
    If a club is banned from UEFA or other European competition:
    Apply: european_competition_ban_modifier = ×0.75 on associated fan token
    Duration: for the full ban period
    Mechanism: loss of UCL/EL revenue; squad quality deterioration; negative narrative
    This is one of the most severe single-event negative modifiers in the library.
    
  PARTIAL COMPETITION BAN (points deduction, transfer restriction, not full ban):
    Apply: partial_sanctions_modifier = ×0.88 (less severe than full ban)

COUNTRY-LEVEL SANCTIONS AFFECTING FAN TOKEN ACCESS:

  International sanctions may prevent holders in certain countries from
  accessing crypto assets including fan tokens.
  
  Apply: sanctioned_jurisdiction_access_modifier = ×0.88 for tokens with
    significant holder concentration in newly sanctioned jurisdictions
  
  HOW TO ASSESS:
    Is the sanctioned jurisdiction in the top-5 holder concentration for this token?
    YES: apply full ×0.88 modifier
    NO (small holder base): apply ×0.96 (marginal)
    Source: OFAC (US), FCDO (UK), EU sanctions lists — official, Tier 1

GEOPOLITICAL EVENTS AFFECTING FIXTURE CALENDARS:

  Tournament relocation:
    When a major tournament is relocated due to geopolitical events:
    Apply: tournament_relocation_modifier = ×0.90 on all associated demand signals
    Mechanism: uncertainty, reduced attendance, changed fan experience
    
  Fixture cancellation:
    If scheduled matches are cancelled due to geopolitical conditions:
    Apply: fixture_cancellation_modifier = remove match-day demand signal entirely
    No match = no match-day PATH_2 event (for $AFC) = no match-day demand spike
    
AGENT RULE:
  Geopolitical signals are the most volatile in the library.
  Always require Tier 1 source confirmation before applying any sanction modifier.
  Never apply geopolitical modifiers based on media speculation.
  Sources: UN Security Council resolutions, OFAC official lists, government press releases.
```
