# Macro — SportFi Regulatory Intelligence

**The regulatory frameworks governing fan tokens, sports digital assets, and
SportFi infrastructure across major jurisdictions. An agent operating in any
market must understand the regulatory context before generating commercial signals.**

Regulatory clarity — or its absence — is a macro-level signal that overrides
individual sport or token analysis. A token that is commercially strong but
operating in a legally uncertain jurisdiction carries systemic risk that no
amount of sporting performance can offset.
