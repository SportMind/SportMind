## CFTC / Kalshi — First Federally Regulated BTC Perpetual (US)

```
MILESTONE:     CFTC_KALSHI_BTC_PERPETUAL_APPROVED
DATE:          2026-05-30
CATEGORY:      FIRST_FEDERALLY_REGULATED_BTC_PERPETUAL_IN_US
REGULATOR:     CFTC (Commodity Futures Trading Commission)
SOURCE:        CFTC.gov — verified primary government source
STATUS:        APPROVED — enduring regulatory milestone

WHAT THIS IS:
  The CFTC approved Kalshi to offer the first federally regulated
  Bitcoin perpetual futures product in the United States.
  A perpetual futures contract has no expiry date — unlike standard
  futures — making it the instrument of choice for institutional
  continuous BTC exposure management.

  Prior to this approval:
    BTC perpetuals were only available on unregulated offshore exchanges
    (Binance, Bybit, OKX etc). No US-regulated venue offered perpetuals.

  After this approval:
    A CFTC-regulated perpetual product creates a new institutional access point.
    Regulated = eligible for pension funds, endowments, bank prop desks.
    The instrument allows institutional traders to maintain continuous BTC
    exposure without rolling quarterly contracts.

WHY THIS IS AN ENDURING MILESTONE:
  Regulatory approvals are permanent. This venue exists regardless of
  subsequent market conditions. The institutional access pathway created
  on 2026-05-30 persists.
```
