## Sovereign wealth fund framework

```
SOVEREIGN WEALTH FUNDS — SIGNAL FRAMEWORK:

  WHY SWF OWNERSHIP CREATES DISTINCT SIGNALS:
    Sovereign wealth funds have indefinite investment horizons — unlike PE or VC,
    they do not need to exit within a fund lifecycle. This creates:
    1. Long-term stability signal — club or asset will not be sold under pressure
    2. Deep financial backing — investment capacity is not fund-lifecycle limited
    3. Geopolitical dimension — investment serves national strategy objectives
    
  SWF PRESENCE MODIFIER:
    Confirmed SWF ownership of a sports club or ecosystem company:
    Apply: swf_stability_modifier = ×1.05 sustained on associated fan token demand
    This reflects reduced financial distress risk and long-term investment confidence.
    
  INVESTMENT VELOCITY SIGNAL:
    When a SWF makes multiple sports acquisitions in a short period
    (3+ transactions in 12 months):
    Apply: swf_velocity_signal = ×1.10 to all associated tokens
    Mechanism: velocity signals sustained strategic commitment, not one-off investment
    This modifier is enduring until investment velocity slows below threshold.
```

## Private equity in sports

```
PE ACQUISITION SIGNAL FRAMEWORK:

  PE ACQUISITION OF A SPORTS CLUB:
    Phase 1 — Acquisition announcement (short-term):
      Uncertainty signal: ×0.95 on associated fan token demand
      Mechanism: PE ownership timeline (3-7 year exit) creates uncertainty about
        long-term commercial strategy; holders may reduce exposure
      Duration: until investment thesis is publicly communicated (typically 2-4 weeks)
      
    Phase 2 — Investment thesis confirmed (growth-oriented):
      Commercial growth signal: ×1.10 sustained
      Mechanism: PE with growth mandate typically increases club revenue and brand investment;
        this is positive for fan token demand through improved club commercial profile
        
    Phase 3 — PE exit:
      Signal depends on acquirer:
        Strategic buyer (another club owner, commercial partner): ×1.05
        Distressed sale (financial pressure forcing exit): ×0.88
        New PE firm acquisition: restart Phase 1 (uncertainty, ×0.95)

PE ACQUISITION OF FAN TOKEN PLATFORM / ECOSYSTEM COMPANIES:

  PE investment in Socios, Chiliz, or related ecosystem companies:
    Positive ecosystem investment signal
    Apply: ecosystem_pe_investment_signal = ×1.08
    Mechanism: institutional capital validates the commercial model;
      PE investment implies expected revenue growth justifying the valuation
    Duration: sustained while PE ownership is active
    
  PE ACQUISITION OF SPORTS TECHNOLOGY COMPANIES INTEGRATING WITH FAN TOKENS:
    Adjacent ecosystem signal — positive
    Apply: adjacent_pe_signal = ×1.04 (smaller; indirect relationship)

PE EXIT SIGNAL:

  PE firm selling its stake in a sports club or ecosystem company:
    Acquirer assessment is critical before applying any modifier.
    Do not apply PE exit modifier until acquirer type is confirmed.
    Apply the appropriate Phase 3 modifier based on acquirer type above.
```

```

  CONFIRMED VC INVESTMENT IN CORE ECOSYSTEM:
    Strong ecosystem confidence signal — VC investors have conducted due diligence
    and believe in the commercial growth thesis.
    Apply: vc_ecosystem_confidence_signal = ×1.10 to overall ecosystem health assessment
    Mechanism: VC investment implies expected revenue growth; smart money validation
    Duration: sustained while VC investment is active in the company
    
  VC INVESTMENT IN SPORTS TECHNOLOGY WITH FAN TOKEN INTEGRATION:
    Adjacent ecosystem signal
    Apply: adjacent_sports_tech_vc_signal = ×1.04 (indirect positive)
    
VC WITHDRAWAL FROM SPORTS CRYPTO:

  DEFINITION:
    Pattern of VC firms reducing or exiting sports crypto investments across
    multiple companies simultaneously — not a single fund exit.
    
  SIGNAL:
    Negative ecosystem sentiment modifier
    Apply: vc_withdrawal_signal = ×0.92 to ecosystem health assessment
    
  CONFIRMATION THRESHOLD:
    Require 2+ confirmed VC withdrawal events in the same sector within 6 months
    before applying the modifier. Single VC exits are normal; pattern is the signal.
    
  AGENT RULE:
    Monitor VC activity as a leading indicator — they conduct due diligence before
    investing or withdrawing, making their activity a forward-looking signal.
    Source: Crunchbase, PitchBook (publicly announced funding rounds).
```
