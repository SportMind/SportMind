## Jurisdiction separation — read before using any UAE source

```
DUBAI (VARA): The Virtual Assets Regulatory Authority governs virtual
  asset activity across the Emirate of Dubai (mainland and free zones),
  excluding the Dubai International Financial Centre (DIFC, which has
  its own regulator, the DFSA). VARA is the regulator referenced
  throughout macro/regulatory/uae.md.

ABU DHABI (ADGM): The Abu Dhabi Global Market operates under its own
  Financial Services Regulatory Authority (FSRA), a SEPARATE framework
  from VARA. ADGM/FSRA treats digital securities using conventional
  securities-regulation concepts, not VARA's virtual-asset category
  system (Category 1 ARVA/FRVA vs Category 2 utility). A VARA approval
  or licence has no bearing on ADGM/FSRA status, and vice versa.

DIFC (Dubai International Financial Centre): A third, separate regime
  within Dubai's own geography — regulated by the DFSA (Dubai Financial
  Services Authority), not VARA. A Dubai mainland/free-zone VARA licence
  does not cover DIFC-based activity.

FEDERAL LAYER: The federal Capital Markets Authority (CMA, which formally
  succeeded the Securities and Commodities Authority on 1 January 2026)
  and the Central Bank of the UAE (CBUAE) both have federal-level
  jurisdiction that can apply alongside any of the above, depending on
  the activity (e.g. CBUAE for AED-denominated payment tokens).

AGENT RULE: When a UAE/Gulf source mentions "the UAE" without specifying
  Dubai, Abu Dhabi, or DIFC, do not assume it means VARA — check which
  regulator and jurisdiction the source is actually describing before
  treating it as relevant to a VARA-governed fan token question.
```

## Source tier classification — UAE-specific

```
TIER 1 (official/primary — library action permitted without cross-reference):
  vara.ae (Dubai Virtual Assets Regulatory Authority)
  rulebooks.vara.ae (VARA's published rulebooks — the authoritative text
    for any classification or procedural question)
  uaepro.league / official UAE Pro League channels (sports property)
  Federal CMA official channels (federal capital markets regulator)
  Central Bank of the UAE (CBUAE) official channels
  ADGM.com / FSRA official channels (Abu Dhabi — separate jurisdiction,
    see Jurisdiction separation above; relevant only if a signal
    specifically concerns Abu Dhabi/ADGM activity)

TIER 2 (corroboration required before library action):
  Specialist UAE/Gulf crypto-law commentary: NeosLegal, KARM Legal
    Consultants (via unlock-bc), Chambers and Partners (Blockchain &
    Crypto-Assets guide), Middle East Briefing, Bitbond, Stobox — useful
    for interpreting VARA/CMA developments pending primary-source
    publication, but always secondary to the Tier 1 sources above,
    especially for anything touching token classification (Category 1
    vs Category 2), since this is an area where secondary commentary has
    been found to compress or oversimplify VARA's own definitions (see
    the ARVA correction in macro/regulatory/uae.md — the error corrected
    there originated from exactly this kind of compression)
  Regional business/sports press: Gulf News, The National, Khaleej Times,
    WAM (Emirates News Agency), Al Etihad — standard Tier 2 corroboration
    rules apply
  Mainstream sports press covering UAE Pro League results, transfers,
    club news: standard global sources already in the source registry

AGGREGATOR / LOW-RELIABILITY (FM1/FM2 apply):
  Social media commentary on VARA licensing speed or Chiliz UAE rumours —
    treat as FAST SIGNAL only, never library action
  Any UAE crypto news source that does not distinguish VARA from ADGM/
    FSRA in its own reporting — treat with elevated scepticism, since
    this conflation is a known failure mode (see Jurisdiction separation)

SPLIT RULE:
  Gulf business press (Gulf News, The National, WAM): confirmed
    licence/approval announcements = Tier 2 · speculative "expected to
    launch" framing = FM2
  Crypto-law commentary firms: analysis of published VARA rulebook text
    = Tier 2 · a firm's own interpretation of ambiguous or unclear
    procedural questions (e.g. Category 2 legal-opinion requirements,
    currently unconfirmed per macro/regulatory/uae.md) = FM1, and should
    not be treated as resolving the open question
```
