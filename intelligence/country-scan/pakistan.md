## Sports-culture context — cricket vs football

```
Cricket is Pakistan's dominant sport by a wide margin, both in
participation and media attention. Football maintains a growing but
distinctly secondary following. This has a direct bearing on how any
future Chiliz/Socios signal from Pakistan should be read:

· A domestic football token (national team or club) would be entering a
  secondary-sport market — demand assumptions from football-primary
  markets (most of the library's existing national/club token base)
  should NOT be applied uncritically to a hypothetical Pakistani
  football token.
· A cricket-linked fan token — via the Pakistan Super League or the
  national cricket team — would be entering Pakistan's genuinely
  dominant sport, and would represent a structurally different demand
  profile than anything currently in the library (SportMind's national/
  club token coverage is presently football-only). No such token exists
  or has been announced as of 2026-09-28.
· This distinction should inform, but not by itself resolve, any future
  signal assessment — always cross-reference macro/regulatory/pakistan.md
  for whether a specific announcement has actually occurred (see that
  file's CRICKET-TOKEN monitoring flag).
```

## Source tier classification — Pakistan-specific

```
TIER 1 (official/primary — library action permitted without cross-reference):
  pvara.gov.pk (Pakistan Virtual Assets Regulatory Authority — official
    news and licensing portal)
  fbr.gov.pk (Federal Board of Revenue — tax authority)
  sbp.org.pk (State Bank of Pakistan)
  Official government/parliamentary sources (Gazette publications,
    Senate committee records — e.g. Senate Standing Committee briefings
    cited in macro/regulatory/pakistan.md)

TIER 2 (corroboration required before library action):
  International crypto-native press with confirmed Pakistan coverage:
    CoinGape, The Block, Decrypt, Cointelegraph, unlock-bc — all have
    published detailed, independently-sourced PVARA coverage and are
    treated as reliable Tier 2 sources for Pakistan regulatory
    developments specifically (per the corroboration standard already
    applied in macro/regulatory/pakistan.md's HP-13 re-verification)
  Pakistani national press: Dawn, The News International, Business
    Recorder, ARY News, Geo News — standard Tier 2 corroboration rules
    apply; useful for domestic political/regulatory context around
    PVARA and FBR developments
  Payments/compliance trade press: thepaypers.com, FinanceFeeds,
    TechJuice — useful for procedural/mechanical detail (e.g. the NOC
    filing process), Tier 2 standard applies
  Adoption/market-sizing sources: Chainalysis Global Crypto Adoption
    Index — Tier 2, treated as the standing library source for
    Pakistan's global adoption ranking

AGGREGATOR / LOW-RELIABILITY (FM1/FM2 apply):
  Social media claims about Socios.com/Chiliz PVARA status specifically —
    treat with elevated scepticism, since NO Tier 1 or Tier 2 source has
    named either company in connection with PVARA filing as of
    2026-09-28 (see macro/regulatory/pakistan.md, HP-13). A social-media
    claim of confirmed status for either company should not be treated
    as resolving HP-13 without independent Tier 1/Tier 2 corroboration.
  General "crypto legal in Pakistan" explainer content (SEO-oriented
    guides, blog aggregators) — often conflates dates and provisions
    across the 2025 ordinance and 2026 Act; cross-check any specific
    date or provision against macro/regulatory/pakistan.md rather than
    treating such guides as authoritative

SPLIT RULE:
  Pakistani national press: confirmed regulatory announcements = Tier 2 ·
    political commentary or speculation about enforcement intent = FM2
  Crypto-native press (CoinGape, The Block, etc.): direct reporting of
    PVARA statements or filings = Tier 2 · market/price commentary
    incidental to the regulatory story = FM1
```
