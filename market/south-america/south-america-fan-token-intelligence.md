## Section 1 — Critical Distinction: Brazilian Calendar Inversion

**THIS IS THE MOST IMPORTANT SINGLE FACT IN THIS FILE.**

The Brazilian domestic football season runs **March–December**
— NOT August–May (European model). This inverts the European
demand pattern entirely.

**Brazilian demand pattern:**

| Period | European Model | Brazilian Model |
|---|---|---|
| August | Pre-season / early season | MID-SEASON · PEAK DEMAND |
| September–November | Season in progress | Title run · HIGHEST DEMAND |
| December | Christmas break | Season concluding |
| January–February | Season in progress | OFF-SEASON · reduced |
| March | Mid-season | Season begins |
| May–June | Title run / playoffs | Mid-season |

**CURRENT WINDOW (August 2026): PEAK DEMAND**

Brazilian fans are maximally engaged in August 2026. This
is not a pre-season or quiet period. It is mid-season for
all nine active Brazilian fan token clubs, with Brasileirão
title implications and Copa Libertadores / Copa Sudamericana
knockout rounds simultaneously active.

Copa Libertadores and Copa Sudamericana run concurrently
with the Brasileirão domestic season (March–December).
Brazilian clubs routinely play domestic AND continental
fixtures within the same high-demand window — compound
signal potential is structurally embedded in the calendar.

**Agent rule 1:** NEVER apply European seasonal demand
assumptions to Brazilian fan token analysis. August = PEAK,
not pre-season. A Brazilian fan token analysis in August
must reflect mid-season engagement, not summer quiet period.

### Domestic — Brasileirão Série A

Brazil's top domestic league. 38-round single-table format.
March–December season.

- Title-run fixtures (September–November): highest domestic
  demand window · multiple fan token clubs in contention
- Relegation battles: distress signal, not demand signal —
  distinguish from positive demand amplification
- Signal weight: STANDARD for most domestic fixtures ·
  ELEVATED during title run and final-day scenarios

### Domestic — Campeonato Estadual (State Championships)

State-level competitions running January–April. Lower signal
weight than Brasileirão or Copa competition.

- Roster rotation is common — starting XI uncertainty high
- Competition tier: below Brasileirão · well below Copa
- Calibration validity: VALID but confidence tier reduced
- Agent rule 3: Apply LOW confidence default for all
  Estadual fixtures. Do not elevate without specific evidence
  of full-strength lineups and competitive stakes.
