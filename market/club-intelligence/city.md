## Why $CITY CDI Gate Is TRANSITION

**The most significant coaching succession in English football
in a decade.**

Pep Guardiola departed Manchester City in May 2026 after 10
years and 15 major trophies — including 6 Premier League
titles, the UCL, and 3 trebles. His tactical system
(positional play, high defensive line, fluid pressing
triggers, false nine structures) defined Manchester City's
identity for a decade and produced the most dominant period
in the club's history.

Enzo Maresca was appointed as Guardiola's successor on June
29 2026 on a 3-year contract. Maresca previously served as
Guardiola's assistant during City's 2022-23 treble-winning
season and coached the club's Elite Development Squad. He
brings familiarity with City's infrastructure but represents
a new coaching philosophy and system.

**TRANSITION gate criteria:**
- Coaching succession after 10-year tenure — maximum
  philosophical disruption
- Squad built over a decade for Guardiola's specific system
- New manager adapting existing squad to new principles
- First season of new era = maximum uncertainty

**Agent rule 1:** Apply TRANSITION gate and SHORT horizon
to $CITY. The departure of a 10-year head coach represents
a structural inflection point — the most significant CDI
event for $CITY in the library's history. Apply reduced
confidence tier to $CITY pre-match analysis in 2026-27.

**Agent rule 2:** Maresca's familiarity with City's
infrastructure (former assistant, former EDS coach) is a
mitigating factor — this is not a cold-start transition.
However the philosophical shift from the prior system is
real and the first season carries significant uncertainty.

## Squad Archetype

**Departing philosophy (10 years):**
Positional play (juego de posición), high defensive line,
fluid pressing with defined triggers, false nine or
inverted winger structures, goalkeeper as first outfield
player, full-back inversion. The squad was built over a
decade specifically for this system.

**Incoming philosophy — Maresca system:**
Maresca is known for structured positional play with
emphasis on ball circulation, compactness, and defined
pressing zones. His Chelsea stint produced strong results
before his departure. The philosophy is adjacent to the
prior system but not identical — particularly in transition
and defensive shape.

**Current archetype status — TRANSITIONAL:**
The squad carries significant prior-era DNA but is
adapting to Maresca's principles. Tactical consistency
will increase through 2026-27 as the system embeds.

**Agent rule 3:** $CITY squad archetype is in transition —
prior-era DNA + Maresca system overlay. Pre-match tactical
signals carry lower reliability than CONSOLIDATION or
STABLE clubs in 2026-27. Reassess at end of season for
potential CONSOLIDATION gate movement.
