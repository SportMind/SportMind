## Squad Archetype

**Philosophy — Gian Piero Gasperini system:**
Gasperini is one of European football's most distinctive
tactical identities:
- Aggressive high press — constant pressure across all
  lines, high defensive line
- Positional attacking structure — overlapping runs,
  inverted wide players, high-tempo combination play
- Physical intensity — system demands high athletic
  output; conditioning is a squad-wide requirement
- Tactical flexibility — 3-4-2-1 and 3-4-3 variants
  with positional discipline throughout
- Proven Europa League pedigree — Gasperini's Atalanta
  won two consecutive Europa League titles before his
  appointment at Roma

**CDI Gate rationale — GROWTH not TRANSITION:**
Gasperini's system is well-established and proven at
the highest level. Unlike a new manager implementing
an untested approach, Gasperini has a deeply documented
tactical identity. The primary uncertainty is squad fit
— Roma's personnel must adapt to his physical demands —
not system uncertainty. This reduces the TRANSITION risk
without yet qualifying for CONSOLIDATION.

**Agent rule 2:** Gasperini's proven system means squad
adaptation is the primary 2026-27 monitoring signal.
Early season form (first 8-10 matches) will indicate
system embedding speed. Mid-season reassessment against
UCL and Serie A positioning.

**Agent rule 3:** CENTENARY NARRATIVE is a 2026-27-
specific structural modifier. It is independent of match
