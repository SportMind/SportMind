requires explicit justification.

## Squad Archetype — Cholismo

**Philosophy:** "Cholismo" — the tactical system defined by
Diego Simeone over 14+ years. Four structural pillars:

1. **Compact defensive block** — 5-3-2 or 4-4-2 shape with
   8+ players behind the ball. Defensive organisation is
   the foundation of every match plan.

2. **Zone-specific pressing** — aggressive pressing only in
   defined trigger zones, not full-pitch pressing. This
   distinguishes Cholismo from high-press systems like Flick
   (Barcelona) or Arteta (Arsenal). Energy is conserved
   for defined pressing moments.

3. **Fast direct counter-attacks** — rapid vertical
   transitions using wide pace once defensive shape is
   broken. Counter-attack efficiency is a primary offensive
   mechanism.

4. **Physical intensity and emotional commitment** —
   "partido a partido" (match by match) mentality. Every
   match treated as maximum intensity regardless of
   competition position. This creates a consistent
   performance floor.

**Agent rule 2:** Cholismo is a STABLE archetype — not a
transitional philosophy. New arrivals adapt to Cholismo.
Cholismo does not adapt to new arrivals. Squad composition
changes within Cholismo do not change the CDI gate unless
Simeone departs.

**Agent rule 3:** Cholismo creates a specific pre-match
signal profile: $ATM consistently compresses space, defends
deep, and transitions rapidly. This is more predictable than
most Tier A opponents — apply higher confidence modifier
to $ATM tactical assessments relative to clubs in TRANSITION
or CONSOLIDATION gates.
