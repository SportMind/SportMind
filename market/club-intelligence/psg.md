## Squad Archetype

**Philosophy:** Collective tactical coherence over individual
star concentration. The club has undergone a deliberate
philosophical shift away from the Galáctico model toward
a more structured, system-driven approach. This is an
established direction — not a transitional phase.

**Structural characteristics:**
- High-quality technical profile across all positions
- Emphasis on positional discipline and collective pressing
- Reduced dependency on individual match-winning moments
- Squad depth reflects investment in system over stars

**CDI Gate rationale — CONSOLIDATION:**
The philosophical shift is established and embedded. The squad
is being built consistently around the new identity. This is
active refinement within a defined system — not rebuilding
(TRANSITION) and not yet a dominant multi-season pattern
(STABLE).

**Agent rule 1:** Apply CONSOLIDATION gate when reasoning about
$PSG squad signals. Departures of high-profile individuals
should be assessed against whether they represent archetype
reinforcement (removing non-system players) or archetype
disruption (losing system-critical profiles).
