## Why $BFC CDI Gate Is TRANSITION

$BFC enters 2026-27 in a structured uncertainty period
driven by three compounding signals:

1. **Manager succession — Italiano departure, Tedesco
   arrival** — Vincenzo Italiano departed by mutual
   termination on May 28 2026 after two seasons that
   delivered Bologna's first UCL participation since
   1964-65 and a Coppa Italia title in 2024-25. His
   departure closes the chapter on the highest peak
   CDI period in the club's recent history. Domenico
   Tedesco was appointed on June 2 2026. Tedesco arrives
   with international management experience (Belgium
   national team, Spartak Moscow, RB Leipzig, Schalke)
   but has not previously managed in Serie A. A new
   system in an unfamiliar league context is the
   primary CDI uncertainty.

2. **Competition step-down — UCL (2024-25) to Europa
   League (2026-27)** — Bologna qualified for UCL in
   2024-25 — their first UCL appearance since 1964-65,
   a 60-year absence. That participation was the highest
   CDI peak in recent history. The transition back to
   Europa League in 2026-27 (after an 8th-place 2025-26
   Serie A finish) reflects domestic consolidation
   difficulty — the UCL peak was not sustained at
   league level. Europa League is a positive competition
   context, but the step-down from UCL represents a
   structural CDI ceiling reduction.

3. **Small stadium capacity constraint** — Stadio
   Renato Dall'Ara has a capacity of approximately
   38,279 with average home attendance in 2025-26 of
   approximately 28,000. This creates a natural ceiling
   on matchday revenue and home atmosphere relative to
   $ASR, $INTER, $JUV, $NAP, and $ACM. The stadium
   constraint is a structural CDI modifier that limits
   compound demand signal amplification at home fixtures
   regardless of competition tier.

**Positive legacy context:** The 2025 Coppa Italia win
(beat AC Milan 1-0 in the final) was Bologna's first
major trophy since 1974 — a 51-year gap. This is a
significant positive legacy signal that sustains fan
base engagement above pre-trophy levels. Supercoppa
Italiana runners-up in 2025-26 reinforces recent
success legacy.

**TRANSITION gate rationale:** Manager succession +
competition step-down + stadium constraint = combined
TRANSITION classification. The positive trophy legacy
prevents a lower gate classification but does not
overcome the structural uncertainty signals.

**Agent rule 1:** Apply TRANSITION gate and SHORT
horizon to $BFC. Confidence tier below GROWTH. Directional
signal only until Tedesco system embedding is confirmed
and competition trajectory is established. Reassess
at end of 2026-27 season.
