## Squad Archetype

**Philosophy:** High-intensity pressing, positional dominance,
youth integration. The Hansi Flick era has established a
clear tactical identity — aggressive gegenpressing triggers,
high defensive line, rapid vertical transitions, heavy
reliance on youth academy products embedded alongside
experienced profiles.

**Structural characteristics:**
- High press with defined trigger zones — ball loss triggers
  immediate coordinated press
- Youth academy integration — significant proportion of
  match squad from La Masia
- Vertical speed — rapid counter-attacking transitions as
  a primary offensive mechanism
- High defensive line — creates space in behind but enables
  sustained pressing

**CDI Gate rationale — CONSOLIDATION:**
The Flick system is established and producing results.
The squad archetype is recognisable and consistent. This is
active refinement within a defined system — not rebuilding
(TRANSITION) and not yet a multi-season dominant pattern
(STABLE).

**Agent rule 1:** Apply CONSOLIDATION gate when reasoning
about $BAR squad signals. New additions should be assessed
for fit with the Flick high-press system — tactically
compatible profiles reinforce the archetype, tactically
incompatible high-profile additions may signal a system
drift.
