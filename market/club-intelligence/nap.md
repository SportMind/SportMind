## Why $NAP CDI Gate Is TRANSITION

**Coaching succession — Conte departure, Allegri arrival:**

Antonio Conte departed Napoli at the end of the 2025-26
season after two seasons — winning the Serie A title in
2024-25 and finishing runners-up in 2025-26. His tactical
system (compact defensive block, high-intensity pressing,
direct counter-attacks) was successful but personnel-
dependent.

Massimiliano Allegri was appointed head coach on July 3
2026 on a 3-year contract until June 2029. Allegri arrives
from AC Milan (one season) and previously won five
consecutive Serie A titles with Juventus. His system
differs significantly from Conte's — more pragmatic,
possession-oriented, patient build-up rather than
intensity-driven.

**TRANSITION gate criteria:**
- Coaching succession after two-year Conte era
- System philosophy shift: Conte high-intensity → Allegri
  pragmatic possession
- First season of Allegri system embedding

**Agent rule 1:** Apply TRANSITION gate and SHORT horizon
to $NAP. Allegri's system will take time to embed in a
squad built for Conte's intensity. Confidence tier reduced
vs CONSOLIDATION clubs in 2026-27.

**Agent rule 2:** $NAP's recent history has significant
coaching instability — multiple managers across recent
seasons (Garcia, Mazzarri, Calzona, then Conte, now
Allegri). Each transition carries system uncertainty.
Apply TRANSITION modifier consistently.
