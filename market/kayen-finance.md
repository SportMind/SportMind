## SECTION 2 — WHAT KAYEN FINANCE DOES

Kayen Finance provides a full DeFi stack built natively on Chiliz Chain. Its confirmed live features are:

**DEX (Live)**
Swap, bridge, wrap, and provide liquidity — all within a single platform. Kayen Finance is the primary venue for on-chain fan token secondary market activity.

**AMM Model (Live)**
Dual AMM architecture:
- Volatile pairs: UniswapV2-style constant product AMM
- Stable pairs: Curve-style low-slippage AMM for correlated assets

Dynamic Directional Fees (DDF): Kayen Finance applies asymmetric fees — different rates for buying vs selling within the same pair. Designed specifically for fan token pair customisation, allowing demand-side pressure to be captured directionally.

**Wrapped Fan Tokens (Live)**
Fan tokens on Chiliz Chain have zero decimal places by design. Kayen Finance wraps fan tokens to 18 decimal places for DEX use. Wrapped tokens (e.g. WGAL, WCHZ) maintain 1:1 value with the underlying fan token and can be exchanged back at any time. This wrapping mechanism enables fractional trading on the DEX.

**Liquid Staking — stCHZ (Live)**
Users can stake CHZ via Kayen Finance and receive stCHZ, a liquid staking derivative. stCHZ represents a staking position and can be used within DeFi while CHZ remains staked.

**Launchpad (Live)**
Chiliz Chain-native launchpad for SportFi projects.

**"Stake to Score" Campaign (Live)**
Active incentive campaign: stake CHZ, earn stCHZ, earn points, compete for a 350,000 CHZ prize pool.

**DEX V3 (NOT YET LIVE)**
A V3 upgrade is planned, introducing concentrated liquidity and capital efficiency improvements. This is not live at the time of writing. Agents must not state DEX V3 features as available until confirmed live.

**Lending Market (NOT YET LIVE)**
A lending market is planned but not yet live at the time of writing. Agents must not state lending market functionality as available until confirmed live.

## SECTION 3 — WRAPPED FAN TOKENS

This section governs agent behaviour when encountering wrapped fan token data.

Fan tokens on Chiliz Chain are issued with zero decimal places. This makes fractional trading on a standard AMM impossible without modification. Kayen Finance resolves this by wrapping fan tokens to 18 decimal places, creating wrapped variants (e.g. WGAL from $GAL, WCHZ from CHZ).

**Key facts:**
- Wrapped fan tokens (WGAL, WCHZ, WPSG, etc.) are DEX trading instruments, not the underlying fan token
- Wrapped tokens are created solely to enable fractional DEX trading on Kayen Finance
- Wrapped tokens maintain a 1:1 value relationship with the underlying fan token at all times
- Wrapped tokens can be unwrapped and redeemed for the underlying fan token at any time
- Wrapped token volume on Kayen Finance reflects secondary market on-chain activity for the corresponding fan token

**AGENT RULE — Wrapped Token Identification:**
Wrapped fan tokens are NOT valid as the primary token identifier in calibration records. A calibration record must reference the underlying fan token ticker (e.g. $GAL, not WGAL; $PSG, not WPSG). Wrapped token DEX volume may be cited as a secondary market signal only — not as primary token identification. When a DEX pair shows WGAL/WCHZ volume, this represents $GAL on-chain activity.

## SECTION 4 — $PEPPER GOVERNANCE ROLE

$PEPPER is the governance token of Kayen Finance.

**Confirmed mechanics:**
- 25% of all protocol fees generated by Kayen Finance are allocated to a $PEPPER buyback and burn program
- $PEPPER holders gain governance rights over Kayen Finance's liquidity incentive program, including decisions on which liquidity pools receive ecosystem incentives
- This makes $PEPPER governance directly relevant to fan token liquidity allocation on Kayen Finance

**Cross-reference:** hp-3-pepper.md for the full $PEPPER token profile, supply structure, and trading signal context.

**AGENT RULE — $PEPPER Governance Caveat:**
$PEPPER governance mechanics are confirmed and real. However, $PEPPER has a memecoin-scale total supply of approximately 8,888 trillion tokens. Agents must not treat $PEPPER as a conventional structured governance token. The governance mechanics (buyback/burn, liquidity incentive votes) are operationally real; the token economics are speculative at this supply scale. When reasoning about $PEPPER, state this distinction explicitly: governance role is confirmed, token economic interpretation is speculative.

## SECTION 5 — stCHZ LIQUID STAKING

stCHZ is the liquid staking derivative issued when CHZ is staked via Kayen Finance.

- Staking CHZ via Kayen Finance yields stCHZ in return
- stCHZ represents a staking position — it is not equivalent to spot CHZ
- stCHZ is a new on-chain CHZ derivative, distinct from CHZ
- stCHZ can be used within DeFi applications while the underlying CHZ remains staked

**AGENT RULE — stCHZ vs CHZ Distinction:**
stCHZ is not CHZ. When reasoning about CHZ supply dynamics, CHZ holder behaviour, or CHZ macro regime signals, agents must not conflate stCHZ positions with CHZ spot positions. stCHZ is a staking derivative position. A holder of stCHZ has an economic interest in CHZ but does not hold CHZ directly. This distinction matters for macro regime analysis: CHZ CAPITULATION readings apply to CHZ spot price and must not be applied directly to stCHZ as if it were equivalent.
