# Commercial Partnership Intelligence

**How commercial partnerships signal club health and affect fan token demand.**

---

## Technology partnership signals

```
MAJOR TECHNOLOGY PARTNER (globally recognised company as official club tech partner):
  Commercial sophistication signal — club has data infrastructure, digital strategy,
  and financial health to attract elite partners.
  CDI modifier: ×1.05 sustained.

CLOUD OR INFRASTRUCTURE PARTNER (major cloud provider for data/infrastructure):
  Analytics sophistication signal.
  Clubs with serious data investment make better long-term decisions.
  Apply: ×1.03 to long-term performance modifier weight.

AI PARTNERSHIP (official club partnership with AI company for performance or fan engagement):
  Forward-looking innovation signal.
  CDI modifier: ×1.04 sustained.
  Particularly relevant for fan token clubs — signals openness to technology-driven
  fan engagement, which is the core fan token value proposition.
```

## Crypto and blockchain partnership signals

```
FAN TOKEN PLATFORM PARTNERSHIP:
  Already covered in fan-token/lifecycle.md and fan-token/ecosystem-health-intelligence.md.
  Cross-reference those files for full fan token platform reasoning.

ADDITIONAL CRYPTO PARTNERSHIP (second crypto/blockchain partner beyond fan token):
  Digital asset openness signal: ×1.05.
  Suggests club is committed to broader Web3 engagement beyond the fan token alone.

CRYPTO PARTNERSHIP TERMINATION (mid-contract, beyond fan token):
  Reassess digital asset openness.
  Pattern signal — multiple crypto partnerships terminated:
  Apply: ×0.90 digital openness modifier.
  May signal regulatory concern or commercial disappointment with the crypto sector.
```

## Retail and merchandise partnership signals

```
GLOBAL RETAIL PARTNERSHIP (merchandise through a major global retailer):
  Fanbase reach signal — club's commercial infrastructure supports global distribution.
  CDI ceiling modifier: ×1.06.
  Addressable holder base extends significantly beyond local geography.

REGIONAL RETAIL CONCENTRATION (merchandise only through regional/local retailers):
  CDI ceiling modifier: ×0.95.
  Primarily local fanbase — lower total addressable holder market.

E-COMMERCE EXPANSION (launch or significant expansion of direct-to-consumer):
  Digital engagement signal: ×1.03.
  Clubs with strong e-commerce infrastructure have better capacity for digital
  product engagement — including fan token utility features.
```

## Partnership health monitoring

```
MULTIPLE PARTNERSHIP TERMINATIONS (2+ major partnerships lost within 6-month window):
  COMMERCIAL_DISTRESS_SIGNAL
  CDI modifier: ×0.82 sustained.
  This is one of the strongest negative commercial signals in SportMind.
  Sponsors and partners have commercial intelligence not publicly available.
  Multiple departures strongly suggest they know something material.

PARTNERSHIP RENEWAL (long-term partner renews and extends):
  Strongest possible commercial health confirmation signal.
  CDI modifier: ×1.08 sustained.
  Extension is harder to get than a new partnership — the partner has full
  information and still chose to continue.

NEW PARTNERSHIP IN DIFFICULT PERIOD (attracts major partner during poor on-pitch form):
  Decoupling of commercial and sporting performance.
  CDI modifier: ×1.05.
  Signals commercial value is not solely dependent on results — CDI resilience.
```
