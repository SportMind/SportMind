# Club Revenue Intelligence

**How revenue stream structure creates enduring financial capacity signals.**
Revenue signals are medium-term context (Horizon 2-3) — not match-day modifiers.

---

## Revenue stream framework

```
THREE PRIMARY REVENUE STREAMS FOR ELITE CLUBS:
  1. Broadcast revenue   — most predictable, determined by league position
  2. Matchday revenue    — stadium-dependent, most stable when full
  3. Commercial revenue  — most variable, fastest growing

Each stream has distinct signal characteristics and CDI implications.
```

---

## Broadcast revenue signals

```
BROADCAST AS LEAGUE POSITION PROXY:
  League finishing position is a reliable proxy for next season broadcast revenue.
  This creates a forward-looking CDI signal from league table position.

PREMIER LEAGUE BROADCAST ALLOCATION:
  Top four finish:       highest allocation
  5th-10th finish:       mid allocation
  11th-17th finish:      lower allocation
  Relegated:             loses PL broadcast entirely — acute financial cliff

UCL PARTICIPATION PREMIUM:
  UCL group stage minimum: ~€15-20m (enduring floor, adjusts with UEFA deals)
  UCL knockout rounds:      escalating per round
  UCL Final appearance:     ~€100m+ total distribution
  Apply: ×1.15 CDI for clubs confirmed in UCL.
  Apply: ×0.85 CDI for clubs dropping from UCL to Europa League.
  Rationale: UCL participation is both a broadcast revenue amplifier and
    a commercial revenue amplifier — sponsorship and kit deal premiums.

BROADCAST-DEPENDENT CLUBS (above 60% revenue from broadcast):
  Relegation creates an acute financial cliff — no parachute covers full loss.
  Apply: ×1.25 weight to relegation signal for broadcast-dependent clubs.
  The financial consequence of relegation is materially worse for these clubs.
```

---

## Matchday revenue signals

```
STADIUM UTILISATION:
  Full stadium (consistently sold out): revenue floor confirmed.
    Apply: ×1.03 CDI — demand certainty signal.
  Consistent empty seats (below 80% capacity):
    Apply: ×0.94 CDI — commercial health concern.
  New stadium or capacity expansion confirmed:
    Apply: ×1.08 CDI sustained for 2-3 seasons.
    Rationale: new stadiums generate elevated revenue through premium seating,
      naming rights, and improved matchday experience — structural uplift.

NEUTRAL VENUE IMPACT:
  European finals at neutral venues remove home matchday revenue from finalists.
  This is an enduring financial structure, not a performance signal.
  Do not conflate neutral venue matchday revenue with competitive performance.
```

## Commercial revenue signals

```
COMMERCIAL REVENUE CHARACTERISTICS:
  Most variable stream — sensitive to club brand and global reach.
  Fastest growing for elite clubs over the past decade.
  Detailed commercial signal coverage:
    See: market/sponsorship-endorsement-intelligence.md
    See: market/commercial-partnership-intelligence.md

COMMERCIAL-DOMINANT CLUBS (above 50% revenue from commercial):
  Most resilient financial model — partially independent of results.
  Apply: ×1.05 CDI stability signal.
  Rationale: commercial-dominant clubs can sustain squad investment
    through short-term performance downturns.
```

---

## Revenue balance signal

```
REVENUE CONCENTRATION RISK:
  Over-reliance on any single stream creates vulnerability.

  Broadcast-dependent (>60%): ×1.25 weight to relegation signal.
  Commercial-dominant (>50%): ×1.05 CDI stability.
  Balanced (no stream >50%): standard CDI — no adjustment.

REVENUE BALANCE AS LONG-TERM CDI SIGNAL:
  Clubs with diversified revenue are more likely to sustain fan token
  demand through performance volatility.
  Clubs with single-stream dependency carry higher CDI volatility.
```

## Prize money as season signal

```
UCL WINNER PRIZE DISTRIBUTION (~€100m+ to winning club):
  Apply: ×1.10 CDI for the following summer transfer window.
  Clubs that win UCL typically reinvest — squad quality signal for next season.
  Duration: elevated CDI applies through the following transfer window close.

UCL RUNNER-UP AND SEMI-FINAL DISTRIBUTIONS:
  Runner-up: ×1.07 CDI transfer window signal.
  Semi-final: ×1.04 CDI transfer window signal.

RELEGATED CLUB FINANCIAL TRAJECTORY:
  PL parachute payments exist but are insufficient to maintain elite squad.
  Parachute payments cover approximately 2 seasons at declining rates.
  Apply: ×0.88 CDI sustained for the parachute payment period.
  After parachute ends: reassess — club may have rebuilt or may have declined further.

DOMESTIC CUP PRIZE MONEY:
  Lower signal weight — cup prize money is insufficient to materially alter
  squad quality signals. Apply: no CDI modifier for domestic cup results alone.
```

## MIND DIMENSIONS

**Intelligence:** Teaches how club revenue streams — broadcast, matchday, commercial — create enduring financial capacity signals that affect CDI, squad building, and fan token demand ceilings.

**Reasoning:** Provides the revenue stream classification framework and how each translates to CDI and transfer window signals — with explicit modifier values for UCL participation, stadium utilisation, and prize money injection.

**Context:** Applies to any analysis where club financial capacity affects squad quality reasoning — transfer windows, relegation risk assessment, and long-term demand trajectory across Horizon 2-3 time frames.

**Memory:** Draws on historical patterns of how clubs that win UCL reinvest prize money and how relegated clubs decline financially through parachute periods — patterns established over multiple seasons across multiple leagues.

**Judgment:** Do not apply specific revenue estimates from stale financial reports — use structural signals (league position, UCL participation, stadium utilisation) as proxies. Never log specific revenue figures as they are expiring operational data.

**Attention:** Revenue signals are medium-term context (Horizon 2-3) — not match-day modifiers. Apply at season level from multi-horizon-reasoning.md. Do not include in pre-match adjusted score calculation.

**Learning:** Prize money and broadcast allocation outcomes are confirmed annually — calibration records can validate CDI modifier values over multiple seasons once a sufficient dataset of outcomes is established.

**Integration:** Revenue intelligence integrates with commercial partnership intelligence, sponsorship intelligence, and ownership intelligence to create a complete financial health picture. Never apply revenue signals in isolation — they are one dimension of the full financial context.
